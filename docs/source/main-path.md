## Every Screen

EVERY SCREEN

### DESIGN

- Top right: empty on every screen, except a night's page, where the "⋯" menu sits: name this night, delete this night
  (founder-ruled 2026-11-07; The Journal). There is no Help icon and no Help page (C147-I; C153-I). The
  phone-and-heart icon and its word, "Help" [founder's draft copy], are kept here as drawn, and are not built.
- Three voices (How the AI Appears §2). The app's own words: the app voice, plain text, centred, no bubble, appearing
  word by word. The dreamer's words: the dreamer voice, in bubbles from the right. Readings: the readings voice, in
  white bubbles from the left (the bubble gradient in the dark theme), each with its tradition's name on a terracotta
  label (C137-I item 4).
- No italics anywhere, except the dreamer's own words when quoted inside a reading or a reflection (C137-I item 2).
- Dates are always day/month/year.
- Motion: word by word for the app's own words. The Word Animation setting turns it off, with the waiting shimmer.
  Screen readers always get each line whole.
- Two themes, light and dark, set by Night/Day mode (Settings). Onboarding is always in the light theme.

### FEATURES AND RULES

- Language: the app opens in the phone's language if it offers it, otherwise English. Changeable later in Settings.
  The app starts in English and Swedish. The founder intends Spanish, and Android, at Cohort 2, and more languages at
  Cohort 3 (Technology Message 8 item 3 [RELAYED]): noted here, nothing planned.
- Settings: three things the dreamer sets for themselves: Language, Word Animation and Night/Day mode (the Settings
  page; the Principles' dated blocks). There is no Region setting: it existed only to choose Help's resources
  (founder-ruled [RELAYED, Head of Product & Design, its brief and message 2 to the Cohesion Filing chat]).
- Feedback: on the Settings page. Never inside a night. Asks about the app, never invites a dream; read by the
  founders; never linked to the install ID or any night; deleted after the cohort's review (C126-I item 4).
- The app never sends notifications and never asks for notification permission (C126-I item 3).
- No sign-up, no account. Nothing is ever shared or exported from the app, except the founder's own export, in his
  Cohort 0 build only (The Journal; C142-I item 3).
- There is no distress check: it is not built and does not run, and nothing about a night is classified for
  distress. Nothing in the app is about distress or help (C147-I; indefinitely, C153-I). The rules that keep a reading from
  hurting anyone are how the readings behave, and stand in full (C147-I).
- The app's face: the name under its icon, "SoulDreams" in both languages (MP·55); the icon is the founder's moon; the
  launch screen is the page colour with the moon (C13-P item 3).
- TestFlight: testers install through it. Its beta description (MP·56) is founder copy, written before Cohort 1. It
  carries W27·10 and W27·11 (C14-P), and asks testers not to share the link (founder-ruled [RELAYED, Head of
  Product & Design, its brief and message 2 to the Cohesion Filing chat]).
- The click-through was run before Cohort 1: one person who is not a beta tester, invented dreams only. No finding
  changed a design (C12-P item 2). Its check of the Help word no longer applies (C147-I).

## Recording

RECORDING (cards at M2, M3, M14)

### DESIGN

- First use: the full instruction at the top. Afterwards: one short line. The founder's first draft, kept as drawn:
  "Press record or type to share your dream. Recordings will be transcribed and deleted, and you can go on to
  questions or save it and keep sleeping." [founder's draft copy] It follows the choice to be read's name (C132-I item
  5); the written instruction is MP·10, the short line MP·32.
- Record button, and the option to type a dream from scratch.
- Recording by voice shows a waveform and a running timer, never a saved file: the recording is deleted
  once transcribed (X9). A screen reader is told a recording is under way.
- The text appears on screen and is editable with a tap, like editing a text message. The record button only ever
  adds text and never takes a command.
- Save (sleeping-person icon): bottom right. Goes back to the journal.
- "Start Interpretation" [founder's draft copy]: bottom left. Goes to the mirror. Save and Start Interpretation have
  equal weight.
- Back arrow: top left. Saves, just like Save.
- Offline: "You're not connected right now, but you can still record a dream. Once you are back online, recordings
  will be transcribed and deleted." [founder's draft copy]

### FEATURES AND RULES

**Recording (O1, O2)**

- Recording asks nothing and adds nothing. Recording and closing the app is a whole use; nothing treats the night as
  unfinished.
- Nothing is detected at recording. The split between dreams is detected when the dreamer taps Start
  Interpretation, because a typed night's words leave the phone only to be read (C140-I item 1; founder-ruled
  2026-11-09). The dreamer first sees it at the mirror, where it is confirmed or undone (C127-I I1).
- Editing the text before being read is the telling itself; no earlier version is kept (C127-I I2).
- The microphone permission is asked the first time they record by voice (C126-I item 3).
- Recording happens in one language: the app's language. A night is read and kept in that language (C122-I item 4).
- The choice to be read is named "Start Interpretation" everywhere (C132-I item 5). It is the one that lets a night's
  words leave the phone to be read (C124-I; C126-I item 1).

**Offline (C126-I item 5, C127-I I5)**

- The recording waits on the phone until it can be transcribed, then is deleted.
- No connection means no reading. Nothing is queued; the app never starts a reading by itself when the connection
  returns.

**When transcription fails (C13-P item 3)**

- With a connection, the recording stays on the phone and is sent again, as when offline, and MP·54 shows. With no
  connection, the offline line (MP·15) shows instead.

**Nights (C128-I item 6; C152-I item 2)**

- A night is dated by its first recording. For the series, an earlier night means an earlier first recording, date
  and time; two nights can share a date.
- The first Save creates the night; later saves update it. The journal shows the date, never the time (C8-P decision
  3).
- A night stays open until the first 21:00 after its first recording, by the phone's local time: a dream at 23:00
  and one at 07:00 the next morning are one night (C152-I item 2, founder-ruled).
- Everything recorded before choosing to be read, and before that 21:00, joins the same night. At that 21:00 the
  recording screen starts fresh; unread nights stay in the journal.
- After a night is read, the next recording starts a new night.
- To add to an older night, open it and record more. A dream added after the mirror is the late telling (C122-I
  item 3).

### WORDS TO WRITE (English and Swedish)

- The top line on first use (MP·10) and the short line after it (MP·32), the offline line (it carries W29), "Save"
  [founder's draft copy], "Start Interpretation" [founder's draft copy] (the choice to be read, EM·1).
- The microphone permission text.
- The no-connection sentence when choosing to be read offline (C127-I I5; EM·8), also shown when a reading cannot
  complete or the beta's spending limit is reached (C13-P item 3).
- The transcription failure line (MP·54).
- What a screen reader is told while a recording runs (MP·33).

## The Mirror

THE MIRROR (cards at M4)

### DESIGN

- Each dream in its own box, with a numbered "Dream n" tag. Until "Looks right", the text in the boxes is in the app's
  voice and font: plainly the app's read-back (C135-I D4). Once confirmed, the dreams show in the dreamer's voice
  everywhere after (How the AI Appears §2.7). The boxes are founder-ruled (C138-I item 2).
- A dream added after the mirror, the late telling, carries its own tag in place of "Dream n", saying it was added
  later, wherever the night's dreams show (MP·51; C138-I item 2).
- Between dreams: move up, merge, move down.
- Tapping the text opens the keyboard, with a scissor to split a dream at that point.
- The read-back can be heard as well as read.
- One line of the app's own, asking only what the mirror got wrong.
- Button: "Looks right" [founder's draft copy], leading into the opening.
- Back to Journal: top left (pauses the session).

### FEATURES AND RULES

- The mirror is the first step of being read, whenever the dreamer chooses to be read; it is never part of recording
  (C124-I).
- Read back in the dreamer's own words (O3). Only filler sounds ("um", "uh") and plain stutters are removed. Any
  repetition that could carry meaning stays; when unsure, it is kept. Grammar, odd phrasing, contradictions and gaps
  stay exactly as said (C128-I item 5).
- It never names the tone, supplies a word, smooths a contradiction or fills a gap (O3).
- Several dreams are shown in order; nothing says how they relate (O4).
- All corrections optional (O5): edit the text, say what is structurally wrong, or give the words to use. Moving,
  merging and splitting happen here, including confirming a split detected when Start Interpretation was tapped
  (C127-I I1; founder-ruled 2026-11-09).
- The app's one line (the 31st place, C125-I item 3) asks only what the mirror got wrong, never for more.
- The heard read-back reads exactly the words on screen, level, adding no feeling and no emphasis (C132-I item 3).
- A screen reader names the mirror as the app reading back (C135-I D4).
- Leaving pauses the session; it resumes exactly where it was left (C128-I item 4).

### WORDS TO WRITE (English and Swedish)

- The mirror's line (31st place), the record line on multi-dream nights (W6), "Looks right".
- The dream tag (MP·34) and the late telling's tag (MP·51), move up and move down (MP·35), and merge and split, with
  the scissor's spoken name (MP·17).

## The Opening

THE OPENING (cards at M4)

### DESIGN

- After "Looks right", the screen slides down. One continuous page; the dreamer can always scroll back up to the
  dreams.
- The app's lines and questions: plain, centred, word by word.
- The dreamer's answers: bubbles from the right.
- Input field at the bottom: type or record.
- Interpret Now: a button just above the input field, from the first thing the opening asks, "what stood out"
  included (C132-I item 2).
- Back to Journal: top left.
- No other buttons. Answers are always typed or recorded.
- Before each new turn of the app's: 20px more space after the last reply (product/design-system.md).

### FEATURES AND RULES

**The order (O12)**

- What stood out → the questions → stance → asking → the bridge → the readings.

**The four framing lines: what stood out, stance, asking, the bridge (C128-I item 1)**

- Composed by the app each night, never the same words twice. They vary in wording only, never in what they point at.
- They echo nothing of the dream, except the bridge, which names its one image in the dreamer's own word.
- They draw on nothing else: no earlier answer, nothing written in the night, no other night. They never suggest an
  answer, option, feeling, meaning or tradition; never name a period of the dreamer's life; never flatter; never speak
  as the dreamer. Each language composed on its own.
- If a composed line fails its check, a founder-written stand-in is shown.
- What stood out: not asked on a night that holds a single image; asked once more at the end if the questions bring
  new images in (O6).

**The questions (O7, O9)**

- Composed by the app each night from what the dreamer has shared. One per turn. "I don't know" is a whole answer.
  They end by themselves when two turns in a row bring nothing new.

**Interpret Now (O8, C128-I item 3, C130-I item 2, C132-I item 2)**

- Goes straight to the readings from any point in the opening; the rest is passed over, and the close follows the
  readings as always.
- Saying "interpret now" does the same when said on its own, or as the very last thing in a response; never inside a
  dream being told. The words are not kept as part of the answer.

**Leaving**

- Back to Journal pauses the session; it resumes exactly where it was left. Not a failed reading (C128-I item 4).

**A failed reading (C127-I I4)**

- One that doesn't finish: the service errors or times out, the connection drops, or the app stops.
- One plain sentence, the failure sentence (C4-P·6), shown alone: when readings fail and none passes, no decline
  shows, and the night stays unread (Lens Practice §14 item 19; founder-ruled 2026-11-07). Only the recording and the
  mirror's corrections stay; nothing else from the session is kept. The dreamer reads again with Start
  Interpretation, which starts at the mirror.
- When the beta's spending limit is reached, the night stays as recorded and can be read later, and EM·8 shows
  (C13-P item 3). A reading that cannot complete shows the failure sentence, never EM·8 (founder-ruled 2026-11-07).

### WORDS TO WRITE (English and Swedish)

- Stand-in lines for the four framing lines.
- "Interpret Now" [founder's draft copy] (W2).
- The failure sentence.

## The Readings & Close

READINGS, BAND & CLOSE (cards at M6, M7, M8)

### DESIGN

**The page**

- One continuous page. The dreamer can always scroll back up through the readings, the opening and the dreams.
- Back to Journal: top left. Same on every screen.
- Input field at the bottom: type or record, for every answer.

**Section labels**

- The Readings and the series question each sit under a section label: a pill in the green gradient, white text. A
  tradition's name sits on a pill in the terracotta gradient, white text. The arc has no section label of its own
  (C138-I item 3).
- The line before the readings (MP·20) sits above "The Readings" label. After the label: 20px, then the image line
  (W8, composed).
- The series question's section label says the tradition asks (AI·11; C135-I D5).
- The waking-life invitation (W17) sits under its heading, MP·53, one fixed founder line (founder-ruled 2026-11-05;
  C13-P item 3).

**Who is speaking, and how it looks** (How the AI Appears §2 and §3)

- The app's own lines (the image line, the invitations, the reflection): plain text, centred, no bubble. Word by
  word.
- The dreamer's words: bubbles from the right, 30px from the left edge and 10px from the right.
- Readings: white bubbles from the left in the light theme, the bubble gradient in the dark theme, 10px from the left
  edge and 30px from the right, each with its tradition's label. All equal in size and weight; nothing ranks them
  (C137-I item 4). Bubble by bubble; each reading arrives whole.
- The arc: the last reading inside "The Readings", the same white bubble and size as every reading. Its terracotta
  label names its kind beside its tradition, "The Arc · {tradition}" (MP·52; C138-I item 3).
- The press: drawn as B1, the app's own look, plain and centred, at its place after the arc. Never set beneath a
  reading, never in a tradition's look, never labelled as a press (C135-I B; C136-I). It cannot be marked (Lens
  Practice §14 items 15 and 16).
- A tradition's decline (W14): in its reading's look, with its label (How the AI Appears §3).
- The series question: asked through a named tradition, so in the readings' look, with its tradition's label, under
  its own section label (C135-I D5).
- The dreamer's words quoted inside a reading: italic, in quotation marks, in the voice they sit in, or in the
  dreamer voice's italic where that face has none. A source's words: upright, in quotation marks, named in the sentence
  (C135-I A; C137-I item 2).
- Before each new turn of the app's: 20px more space after the last reply.

**Controls**

- Next: at every invitation in the close. One tap, takes them to the next invitation. Never takes them past the
  waking-life invitation.
- Add to Journal (W24): appears with the waking-life invitation and stays through the reflections. Ends the session and
  keeps the night.
- Marking: tap a sentence in a reading, its quoted line, or the arc to mark it. The mark stays visible in the night.
  The press, the series question and the invitations can't be marked (Lens Practice §14 item 16).
- No progress shown anywhere, and no count of what's left.

**In the reflections**

- The reflection is one paragraph in the app's voice, composed each night (C137-I item 1). Nothing more to draw.
- The space's state (W21) is a line above the input field, in the button style. The empty case (W22) stands where a
  reflection would, in the app's voice. Both are sentences (C137-I item 3).

### FEATURES AND RULES

**The order (C129-I item 2)**

- The image line → the readings → the arc → the press → the series question → the dreamer's response → the
  waking-life invitation → the reflections.

**The readings (L1 to L9)**

- First, one line names the image the readings stand in front of, as where the dreamer's own attention went (W8). If
  no tradition reads that image, it says so and names the one read instead (W9). Both are composed (C134-I item 1).
- The line before the readings: the founder's first draft, kept as drawn: "Here are the most relevant traditions
  based on what you have shared today. Remember, it's what you make of it that matters." [founder's draft copy]
  C132-I item 1 removes "most relevant" and "today"; the written line is MP·20.
- At most three readings, set by tonight's material alone (L3). Each is one tradition under its own name (L9), with a
  line quoting the dreamer's own words (L6) and one paragraph per dream it can ground (L5). A dream with nothing left
  open, or not reached, is told so (L4).
- The readings never ask and never speak in hypotheticals (C134-I item 2).
- A tradition that cannot read from what was told declines, in its own voice, composed each night, with no stand-in
  (W14; Lens Practice §15 item 11; C149-I Q13). If composing the decline fails, the next tradition with purchase
  reads, or the slot is empty (C149-I Q6, Q13).
- When every chosen tradition declines, the night shows the declines themselves, each in its reading's look with its
  label, and nothing else marks it: no new place and no new words (Lens Practice §14 item 19; founder-ruled
  2026-11-07). What follows such a night is asked of Interpretation (C16-P).
- The order means nothing, and nothing says what it means (L7). It never changes when the night is reopened (L8).

**The band**

- The arc: on some nights with several dreams, one last reading about how the dreams sit together, in one tradition's
  voice. On other nights, nothing marks its absence (L16).
- The press: at most once; the dreamer never sees it as a step (L17). Composed, with no fixed line: if it fails twice,
  it does not show that night, and the failure is recorded (C140-I item 5). No part of the app ever says a reading is
  true, right or the one (C136-I).
- The series question: on the few nights it runs, about what has returned, through a named tradition (S2, W18). On
  other nights, nothing says it's missing (B3). Composed, with no stand-in: if composing fails, it is silent that night
  (founder-ruled 2026-11-03; notice to Interpretation).

**Marks (C129-I item 3)**

- The close may use marks tonight only. Never read by a later night, never counted per dreamer, and the readings never
  change because of them.
- The app never treats a mark as agreement, and never gives more of the same because of one.
- The reflection may place a marked sentence, and never says it was marked (The Close Draft 5, H8; C150-I).
- The marking line (MP·24) stays as the founder wrote it, its unit included, and nothing about the close is added:
  a dreamer who has not reached the close does not know what the close is (founder-ruled 2026-11-07 [RELAYED, Head of
  Product & Design]). Interpretation is asked to bring C129-I item 3, Lens Practice §9B, §14 item 16 and §15 item 15,
  L18 and The Close to the line (C16-P).

**The invitations (C129-I items 1, 5)**

- Composed by the app each night: what stood out, what the dreamer makes of the night, then the waking-life
  invitation.
- They name no image, reading, tradition or time; suggest no answer, feeling or meaning; draw on no earlier answer and
  no other night. Each language composed on its own.
- If a composed line fails its check, a founder-written stand-in is shown.
- Responding is the path the design encourages, but never through a consequence: nothing rewarded, nothing withheld.
  Moving on never reads as skipping.

**The waking-life invitation (C129-I items 1, 2)**

- Met on every night that is read. Nothing passes over it.
- Asks about waking life; never names a time ("the day", "yesterday"). Leaves both doors equally open: "all of it"
  and "none of it" are equally whole.

**The reflection (C129-I item 4; C137-I item 1)**

- One paragraph in the app's voice. Starting from what the dreamer wrote, it quotes their words beside specific lines
  from the dream, the readings, the arc, the series question and their marks, as many as the night supports, each
  quoted word for word, in quotation marks, with where it comes from named.
- The app's own words in it say only where and when. Never a word that names a relation between the quoted lines.
- The dreamer's words come first: it begins from what they wrote, never from the night.
- Their quoted words are in italics, and each quoted line of theirs says whether it is from the dream or from what
  they wrote after the readings (C137-I item 2).
- It never says what anything means, never praises or characterises the dreamer, never asks, and ends in a full stop.
- It never uses what they said at the stance, the asking, the bridge or the waking-life invitation.
- Composed freely (C140-I item 3), built to C137-I item 1. When it fails its check, the closed-set form stands in: the
  founder's where-and-when phrases (AI·12 to AI·16) and everything else quotation. AI·17 is retired: the reflection
  never says a line was marked (The Close Draft 5, H8).
- Then the app's part ends, and the night stays open and quiet. A line says in the moment whether the app will still
  respond (W21).

**Ending**

- If the dreamer never taps Add to Journal, the app's part ends by itself a day after their last action. Nothing is
  announced (C7).
- The night is then kept exactly as it was shown (C8). Only the space stays writable.
- The whole night stays above: the look back (C1). The dream in it shows in the dreamer's voice (C135-I D4).

### OPEN

## The Journal

THE JOURNAL (cards at M3, M9, M10, M11)

### DESIGN

1. The journal list (the app's home)

- Top left: the way in to Settings.
- Top right: empty (founder-ruled 2026-11-07).
- Top: the search field, 50px tall, placeholder "Search your dream journal" [founder's draft copy] in tinted text.
- The list: one row per night, newest first. No row ever shows a time.
  - An unnamed night's row: its date (h3), then the first dream's opening line. It never shows an empty name slot or
    an invitation to name it (C138-I item 1).
  - A named night's row: its name (h5), the date under it in the button style, then the first dream's opening line.
  - The row's summary is always three lines.
- Named and unnamed rows look different (founder-ruled; C138-I item 1 replaces C131-I item 1's "look alike"). A read
  night still looks like an unread one (S5).
- Month dividers: in the current year, the month alone ("October"); in an earlier year, month and year ("October
  2026"). No count on any divider; no divider over a month with no nights (C131-I item 2).
- The list scrolls clear of the record button, with space below the last row.
- Bottom centre: the record button, the biggest thing on the screen: a terracotta gradient, "Record dream" in white.
- Empty journal: "This is where your journal entries will appear." [founder's draft copy] and the record button.

2. Inside any night

- The header: the date as h2, or the name as h2 with the date under it in the button style; 20px above and 10px below,
  in both themes.
- The "⋯" menu, top right, where Help was (founder-ruled 2026-11-07): name this night, delete this night. Naming is
  never asked for and never prompted. Deleting has one plain confirmation. In the founder's own Cohort 0 build only,
  it also holds his export.
- The dreams sit under the section label "Dreams".

3. A night opened, not yet read

- The dreams, in the dreamer's words, editable.
- Record more: adds a dream to this night.
- Start Interpretation: the choice to be read. Goes to the mirror. Offered, never nudged.
- The space below the dreams: the dreamer can write here; the app answers with a reflection in the same composed
  paragraph, in the app's voice, as the close's (C137-I item 1; C138-I item 4).
- Back to Journal top left.

4. A night opened, read

- It opens at the bottom, at the space, with the night above.
- The night's own flow at rest: none of the reading's controls, no motion. The dreams in the dreamer's look (C135-I
  D4), the waking answer marked as waking, every voice in its own look, the header, the space. Their marks visible.
- The space at the bottom: always writable. The respond-state line (W21) just above it.
- If the session is still open: Add to Journal.
- No "Record more": a read night takes no new dreams.

5. A night paused in the opening

- Opening it resumes exactly where it was left. Looks the same as any other night in the list.

6. Search

- Searches everything the dreamer wrote, across the whole journal, the space and night names included. A word or a
  phrase.
- No suggestions, no autocomplete, no recent searches.
- Results: the nights containing it, newest first. Each result shows the date and, in the line's place, the passage of
  the dreamer's own words around the first place the word appears in that night, with it highlighted. No ranking.
- Tapping a result opens the night with the word highlighted. The highlight is gone when they leave the night.

**From the close to the journal**

- Add to Journal: the session ends, the night is kept, and the dreamer stays on the night, which now shows it is
  entered. Nothing celebrates, nothing animates.
- Back to Journal during the close: back to the list. The night stays open; the app can still reflect if they return.
  Leaving the app is always allowed.
- If they never tap Add to Journal: the app's part ends quietly 24 hours after their last action in that night,
  whatever the date.

### FEATURES AND RULES

**The list (S5, C9, C131-I items 1, 2)**

- The app shows the dreamer's words in the dreamer's order, and never chooses among them by importance.
- The night's line: the first dream's opening as told, verbatim, with only the framing left off ("So tonight I had a
  dream that…"). When unsure, nothing is left off. A named night shows its name as well, above the date.
- A name is never read by a reading or a later night. Search finds it.
- A read night looks like an unread one.
- Never shows which nights they wrote in, how long they stayed, or which nights connect to others. No "last updated".
- Never: a badge, status, count, thread, link, highlight, tag or filtered view. No count of dreams per night. No
  titles the app writes.

**An unread night (C10, C127-I I3, C128-I item 6)**

- Whole as it is; nothing treats it as unfinished.
- Its space answers: reflections begin on the first write. Writing there never closes the choice to be read, and the
  readings never read it.
- The dreamer adds a dream by opening the night and recording more; the app never guesses.
- It can be read any time later; the mirror comes first.

**Adding dreams once questions have started (C122-I item 3; for Cohort 1)**

- During the opening, a dream can still be added: it goes after the dreams already mirrored, in the order told, with
  no second mirror.
- After the readings, no new dreams; the next recording starts a new night.

**A read night (C1, C8, C12, S4)**

- Kept exactly as it was shown, the series question included. Nothing recomputed, regenerated or reordered. The dream
  shows in the dreamer's voice (C135-I D4).
- The space stays writable forever; on an entered night, a write is kept and nothing engages with it.
- The line above the space has three states: the app will reflect / the app's part is done / this night is entered
  (W21).
- A thin night's look back is short because the night was short (C11).

**Search (S6, S7, C131-I item 3)**

- The dreamer's own words only, never a reading's or the app's.
- The word as typed and its plain forms; never a synonym or a related word. A capital letter does not make a different
  word (C151-I).
- Results in date order, never by relevance. Within a night, the first place the word appears, never the best one.
- A word found on one night only: the result list shows it by itself, with no line (C134-I item 6). Nothing found:
  says so without implying they searched wrongly (W25).
- Never suggests, completes or lists a word. Never proposes a connection or a pattern.

**Across nights (S2, S5, S8)**

- What returns reaches the dreamer only as the series question, inside a night. No view of patterns.
- Nothing written after the readings changes any later night.

**The founder's own export (C13-P item 3; C142-I item 3; C147-I)**

- In the founder's Cohort 0 build only: a button in each night's "⋯" menu sends that night from his phone to the
  restricted folder, never through a server or any service. Its label is his, seen only by him. No tester build has it.
- Exported nights are read only in Interpretation's study chat (C142-I item 3). The export carries no distress results:
  none exist (C147-I).
- Before an update changes how the app stores the journal, the founder may use the same button to export his nights
  as a safety copy, one night at a time, deleted once the update is checked. No import is built: it keeps his words but
  cannot restore his journal (C153-I item 3).

**Deleting (X11, C122-I item 2)**

- A night, from its "⋯" menu, or the whole journal, from the Settings page, any time, with one confirmation each.
  Really gone, with everything made from it. Removing the app removes every night.

## Settings

SETTINGS (cards at M0)

### DESIGN

- Reached from the journal, top left. The title in h2. Back: top left.
- A quick light/dark toggle at the top centre. Tapping it while Night/Day mode is Automatic switches it to Manual.
- Three settings:
  1. Language.
  2. Word Animation: On or Off. Off stops the app's own words appearing word by word, and the waiting shimmer.
  3. Night/Day mode: Automatic, or Manual (Day or Night, Day first). Automatic turns dark at 21:00 and light at
     08:00, by the phone's local time. If the change happens while the app is open, it fades over five seconds; with
     Word Animation Off or the phone's "Reduce motion" on, it changes at once.
- Feedback: its title (MP·49), a short intro, the field, Submit. In the dark theme, Submit is the light button with
  #333333 text (product/design-system.md). Its line says that feedback is never linked to the journal, is read by
  our team, and is then deleted. For Cohort 1 the team is the founders.
- Beside the settings: "Read disclosure & privacy", the disclosure re-readable, with no consent asked; and "Delete
  journal", set apart, in the destructive style, with one confirmation.

### FEATURES AND RULES

- Settings hold only what the dreamer sets for themselves and never change how the practice works (the Principles'
  dated blocks).
- The disclosure, feedback and deletion sit on this page and are not settings.
- Feedback keeps C126-I item 4's rules: it asks about the app and never invites a dream; read by the founders; never
  linked to the install ID or any night; deleted after the cohort's review.
- There is no Region setting (founder-ruled [RELAYED, Head of Product & Design, its brief and message 2 to the
  Cohesion Filing chat]).
- What the dreamer sets here stays on the phone.
