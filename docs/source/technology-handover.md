**What kind of document this is.** A Playbook: current method, meant to change. When a line changes, this file is
replaced and the old text archived verbatim. Whether it is filed is stated in product/product-change-record.md, never
here. Kit item 9: design plus behaviour.

**Whose it is.** The founder's. Head of Product & Design wrote it at his request on 2026-11-05 [RELAYED, the founder]
from the documents it names, and its next version at C13-P, carrying Interpretation's and Technology's corrections and C142-I, the layered disclosure at C14-P, and at C15-P

holds each answer. It never restates a rule in full: a rule living in two documents is a future rift.

## 3. Screen by screen

Each line names what is easiest to miss. The page in main-path.md holds the rest.

**Every screen** (main-path.md, Every Screen). The back control top left. The top right is empty, except on a night's
page, where the ⋯ menu sits (founder-ruled 2026-11-07). No Help icon and no Help page (C147-I). Nothing ever reaches
out: no notification, no badge, no message from the app outside a session (Principles §4i).

**Onboarding** (main-path.md, Onboarding). Always in the light theme. Welcome, the founder's note, the age question
(bands, under 18 ends; the band never leaves the phone, C146-I item 1), the disclosure in two layers with one consent (MP·8). Onboarding shows the first layer:
W27·1, W27·2, W27·5, W27·13 to W27·15 and W27·17, then MP·57, the link MP·58 and the consent line MP·59. MP·58 opens the full
disclosure, the disclosure and privacy page in Settings (MP·48), before the consent. Every other part shows only
there; W27·10 and W27·11 also stand in the TestFlight description. Nothing about distress or help appears in onboarding,
or anywhere in the app (C147-I). There is no instructions screen, and no language
switch on the first screen: the app follows the phone's language, and Settings changes it (founder-ruled
2026-11-06).

**Recording** (main-path.md, Recording). The first-use line (MP·10) once, then the short line (MP·32). Type or record;
a voice recording shows its sound as it is made and a running timer, never a saved file. Offline, recording still works and waits
for a connection to be turned into text (MP·15). If transcription fails with a connection, the recording stays on the
phone and is sent again, and MP·54 shows. Save and Start Interpretation have equal weight. A night is dated by its
first recording and stays one night until it is read, or until the first 21:00 after its first recording, by the
phone's local time (C152-I item 2; main-path.md, Recording). Nothing is detected at recording: the split between
dreams is detected when Start Interpretation is tapped (founder-ruled 2026-11-09).

**The Mirror** (main-path.md, The Mirror; How the AI Appears §2.7). The read-back is the dreamer's own words as told,
person and tense as said, not shortened, with only filler sounds and plain stutters dropped: never a retelling (C128-I
item 5; C130-I item 2). It is in the app's voice until "Looks right"; after that the dream shows in the dreamer's voice
everywhere. Each dream in its box with its "Dream {n}" tag;
move up, merge, move down between boxes; tapping the text opens the keyboard with the scissor to split. A dream added
later carries MP·51 wherever the night's dreams show (C138-I item 2).

**The Opening** (main-path.md, The Opening). Interpret Now from "what stood out", the first thing the opening
asks (C132-I item 2; W2), as a button or spoken (TECH·2). The waiting labels with the shimmer (How the AI Appears §5).

**The Readings & Close** (main-path.md, The Readings & Close; How the AI Appears §3). The image line, then the readings
in white bubbles (the bubble gradient in the dark theme), each with its tradition's label; the arc is the last
reading inside "The Readings", its label MP·52 (C138-I item 3). The dreamer's quoted words in italics; a source's words
upright, named. A tradition's decline (W14) in its reading's look, with its label. When every chosen tradition
declines, the night shows the declines and nothing else marks it; when readings fail and none passes, only the
failure sentence shows (C4-P·6), no decline, the night stays unread, and Start Interpretation reads it again (Lens
Practice §14 item 19; founder-ruled 2026-11-07). The press in the app's voice after the arc, unlabelled. The press, the
series question and the invitations cannot be marked (Lens Practice §14 item 16); the marking line (MP·24) says
nothing about the close (founder-ruled 2026-11-07). The series question with its label (AI·11). The close's
invitations, Next, the waking-life invitation under its heading, "Waking Life" (MP·53, a fixed founder line over W17,
founder-ruled 2026-11-05), the reflection, "Add to Journal" (W24), and the line above the input field (W21, W22). A night
never added is still kept, and nothing may suggest otherwise (C141-I item 5).

**The Journal** (main-path.md, The Journal). Rows: an unnamed night's date, then its opening with the framing trimmed
(TECH·1); a named night's name, the date under it, then the opening. No row shows a time or an empty name slot.
Month dividers: the month alone in the current year, month and year before it. Search on the phone, plain forms only
(C140-I item 6); a capital letter does not make a different word (C151-I). A read night opens at the bottom, at the space. The night's menu (⋯), top right, holds name and delete.

**Settings** (main-path.md, Settings; the Principles' dated blocks). Three settings: Language, Word Animation,

app is open. Feedback on the same page. "Read disclosure & privacy" (MP·48) opens the whole disclosure. Delete
journal, set apart, one confirmation.

## 4. The words

Every dreamer-facing word is in product/places-register.md and .csv, written by the founder in each language
independently. The build never writes, translates or rewords a line.

- **Forms.** " ¶ " is a paragraph break inside a line. A slot in braces is filled by the app: {n}, {tradition},
  {pointed image}, {read image}. " / " separates a place's several short lines; its flag says where each
  shows.
- **Places in parts.** The disclosure (W27·1 to W27·17; W27·7 is merged into W27·6, and W27·16 is retired). W26 and
  its parts, the Help page, are retired indefinitely (C147-I; C153-I).
- **Statuses that change the build.** "Composed (stand-in written)": the app composes the line, and the founder's line
  shows if composing fails. "Composed, no stand-in": nothing shows on failure (W16, W18), or the next tradition reads
  (W14, §5). "Paused", "blocked", "retired": no line is built. "To rewrite": the line stands, and is built as it
  is, until the founder rewrites it (W27·2, W27·3, W27·6, W27·10, W27·17, AI·12, and W1's stand-in).
- **The checks.** product/founder-lists-for-technology.md: each list checks only the lines its section names, and
  quotations are never checked.

|---|---|

| The reflection | The closed-set form: AI·12 to AI·16 and quotation only (C140-I item 3); AI·17 is retired |
| A tradition's decline (W14) | The next tradition with purchase reads; if none, the slot is empty (C149-I Q6, Q13) |
| The press (W16) | Composed again once; then it does not show that night, and the failure is recorded (C140-I item 5; C141-I item 2) |
| The series question (W18) | Silent that night, with nothing shown |
| A source quotation | The reading composed again without any quotation; the tradition replaced only if that fails (C140-I item 4) |

When the technology fails, not a composed line (Technology Message 5 item 1; founder-ruled 2026-11-05):

| Failure | What happens |
|---|---|
| Transcription breaks off or fails | The recording stays on the phone and is sent again, as when offline; MP·54 shows |

| The beta's spending limit is reached | The night stays as recorded and can be read later; EM·8 shows |

## 9. What the app never has

Principles §4: no chat thread, no variable reward, no onboarding built for activation, no implicit memory of the
dreamer, no testing on dreamers, no growth surfaces, no export in any tester build, no lock-in, no reaching out. Also: no
account before Cohort 3, no install ID in Cohorts 0 and 1 (C141-I item 1), no view of patterns across nights, and no
per-person trend, score or measure (the law document §4; C140-I item 9). There is also no distress check and no Help
page (C147-I; C153-I).

**The founder's own export.** In the founder's Cohort 0 build only, a button in each night's ⋯ menu sends that night
from his phone to the restricted folder, never through a server or any service. Its label is his, seen only by him.
Technology may build it, for that build only (C142-I item 3). The export carries no distress results: none exist (C147-I).
Before an update changes how the app stores the journal, the founder may use the same button for a safety copy of his
nights, one night at a time, deleted once the update is checked; no import is built (C153-I item 3). Exported
nights are read only in Interpretation's study chat, never in a Product or Technology chat; restricted/ stays outside
the repository, and outside every folder a Claude Code session is opened in.
