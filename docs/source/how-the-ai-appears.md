## 1. What the app is on screen

**The app is not a person.** It has no name, no avatar, no face, no icon beside its words and no persona in its look
(the Principles §4a; C135-I D1 (d)). Nothing shows the app "typing": no dots, no "is typing", no cursor. What shows
while it works is §5, and it is a label, never a sign of someone writing.

**No voice on a night's page implies a person behind it.** No signature, portrait, handwriting or human name sits on
any voice. The handwritten note and its signature are the founders speaking, in onboarding, before any night
(product/main-path.md, Onboarding). They never appear on a night's page.

**How the app speaks is Interpretation's,** ruled at C135-I D1 under the founder's positions. This document decides
only what shows.

**Saying it is an AI.** The tester disclosure says once, plainly, that an AI composes what the app says each night: its
questions, the readings and the reflections (C135-I D2). Its words are W27's, and main-path.md's fact list carries the
sentence; whether the law requires more is counsel's. Nothing on screen says it again in every turn. Asked what it is,
the app says plainly that it is an AI, in a fixed line (AI·9). Thanked or praised, it never takes the credit and never
praises back; at most once a night it may say the sense is the dreamer's own, in a composed line (AI·10). Otherwise it
goes on with its job (C135-I D1 (d)). Both lines take the app's look: plain, centred, no bubble, no label.

**How the Principles are read here.** §4a refuses the chat thread and a persona. It does not refuse motion. §4b gives
the look of the app's work to this document. So no dated block in the Principles is owed for what §4 and §5 decide
[RELAYED, Head of Product & Design, its message 3, item 2]. One was owed for the voices: §4a counted four, and its
dated block of 2026-10-23 makes them three (C135-I A).

## 2. The three voices

A night has three voices, and each must be recognisable (C122-I item 6, as C135-I A revises it; the Principles §4a and
its dated block). The founder's "the tradition speaking" and Interpretation's "the app through a named tradition" are
one voice, under two names. A quoted source is not a voice: it sits inside the tradition's reading, as a quotation
(§2.3).

| Voice | Its type (design-system.md) | Container | Alignment | Label | Colour role | How it arrives | A screen reader is told |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The dreamer | Dreamer voice | Bubble | From the right | None | The bubble: white in the light theme, the bubble gradient in the dark theme; never the bare page | As typed or transcribed; nothing animates it | That the words are the dreamer's (EM·10). Paused for Cohorts 0 and 1: only what is visible is heard (§8) |
| The app | App voice | None: plain text. The mirror alone sits in boxes (§2.6, §3) | Centred. The mirror alone is a left-aligned column (§3) | None. The app has no name | The page's own text colour, on no surface; the mirror's boxes alone (§3) | Word by word while Word Animation is on (§4); whole when it is off | That the app is speaking; at the mirror, that the app is reading back (EM·10; C135-I D4). Paused for Cohorts 0 and 1: only what is visible is heard (§8) |
| The tradition (the app through a named tradition) | Readings voice | Bubble, the same for every tradition: white in the light theme, the bubble gradient in the dark theme (C137-I item 4). A quotation sits inside it, set apart, its source named (§2.3) | From the left | The tradition's name, on a pill in the terracotta gradient (W13) | The same bubble as the dreamer's; told apart by its voice, its side and its label, never by colour alone | Whole, one bubble at a time | The tradition's name, then the reading; a quotation as a quotation, with its source (C4-P·1; EM·10) |

**2.1 Equal weight.** Every tradition has the same bubble, the same terracotta label, the same size rules and the same
spacing. Nothing ranks a reading: no size, weight, emphasis, colour, position, badge or first-one-larger (C122-I item
6; C137-I item 4). The order carries nothing, and nothing in the look says what it means (L7; P1). No tradition has a
colour or icon of its own: its label carries it. A tradition looks the same on every night.

**2.2 A tradition is a way of reading, never a person.** No portrait, figure, character or symbol of a person sits
beside a reading. Its label is the tradition's name and nothing else.

**How a tradition is recognised** (C135-I C). First by what it says: its account of what a dream is, its vocabulary,
its characteristic question. Idiom only from the tradition's own vocabulary and sources, never as a costume: no
archaic, exotic or mock-sacred diction. Equal weight reaches tone: no tradition more vivid, more certain or more
intimate with the dreamer than another. A night is read in one language (C122-I item 4). These are rules of the
readings' words, and Interpretation's. The look adds nothing to them: no tradition has a typeface, ornament or
decoration of its own, which would be a costume in the look.

**2.3 Quotations inside a reading.** A reading holds two kinds of quotation.
- **The dreamer's own words.** The why-line and each paragraph quote them, and the quotation stays separable from the
  tradition's prose (L6). Every reading shows which of the dreamer's words it stands on (C135-I D1 (c)). They are set
  in italics, in quotation marks, in the voice they sit in (C137-I item 2; founder-ruled). If that face has no true
  italic, they are set in the dreamer voice's italic instead. [ASSUMED] The readings voice's face has no true italic;
  the founder checks it in Framer, and both cases stand here until he does.
- **A source's words.** Quoted word for word only, always named in the sentence, upright, in quotation marks, so the
  dreamer can tell the source's words from the tradition's (C135-I A; C137-I item 2). The source's name sits with its
  quotation, never only somewhere else.
Italics and quotation marks tell each kind from the tradition's prose and from the other kind. Neither rests on colour
(WCAG 2.2, SC 1.4.1) [RELAYED, not read this session], and a screen reader must be able to tell each kind from the
prose and hear the source named. The why-line's prefix is W12, blocked.

**2.4 The dreamer's waking words.** In the look back, the dreamer's answer about waking life is marked as waking
(C122-I item 6). It stays the dreamer's bubble. Its marking never dims, greys, strikes or judges it. Quoted in the
reflection, it says that it is waking (C137-I item 2; §3).

**2.5 The mark.** A mark sits on a claim, never on a question (C122-I item 6). The series question and the
invitations cannot be marked (L18), and neither can the press (Lens Practice §14 item 16): on them there is simply no
way to mark, never a control shown as unavailable. A
mark is visible, never by colour alone, and the same on every tradition. Nothing about the readings changes because of
a mark (L18, as C129-I item 3 reads it). The reflection may quote a marked sentence beside the dreamer's words tonight
(C129-I item 3; C137-I item 1), and never says it was marked (The Close Draft 5, H8).

**2.6 The app's look is never a reading's.** No line of the app's takes a bubble, the terracotta or a tradition's
label, and no reading takes the app's look. Two things are drawn that are not a reading's look, and must never be
taken for one:
- **The mirror's boxes.** Until "Looks right", each dream sits in a box, in the app's voice and face: plainly the app's
  read-back (C135-I D4; founder-ruled, C10-P). This replaces "the mirror is plain text, no bubble". It goes to
  Interpretation as a notice.
- **The section labels.** Pills in the green gradient, naming a part of the night: The Readings, the series question,
  Dreams. They name where the dreamer is, never what a part means, and never a tradition. The arc has none (C138-I
  item 3).
If either could be taken for a reading's look, W17's heading reopens (C133-I item 3).

**2.7 The dream, once confirmed, is the dreamer's.** Until the dreamer confirms it, the mirror is the app's
read-back of their dream, in the app's look (C135-I D4). Once confirmed, the dream shows in the dreamer's voice
everywhere after: the dreamer's words quoted in the readings, the look back, and the journal. Inside a reading's bubble
and in the reflection, that voice is carried by italics in quotation marks (§2.3), never by colour alone.

**2.8 Everywhere.** No italics anywhere, except the dreamer's own words quoted inside a reading or a reflection
(C137-I item 2; product/main-path.md, Every Screen). Colour never carries meaning alone. Text and labels meet the AA
contrast floor (the Principles §3). The look is the same in both themes, colour aside.

## 3. Where each part of a night stands

In the order a night runs (product/main-path.md; C129-I item 2). Only the look is set here.

**The mirror.** Each dream in its own box, numbered with a "Dream n" tag (MP·34), in a left-aligned column, in the app's
voice and face (§2.6; C135-I D4). Between dreams, controls to move up, merge and move down (MP·35, MP·17); tapping the
text opens the keyboard, with a scissor to split a dream there (MP·17). Until the dreamer confirms it, it is the app's
version of their dream, which is why they check it, and the look says so truly (C135-I D4, which revises C132-I item
8's note on it). Once confirmed, the dream is the dreamer's (§2.7). A screen reader names the mirror as the app reading
back (C135-I D4; C127-I I12). The mirror's line (C125-I·3) is the app's: plain, centred.

**The late telling** (MP·51). A dream added after the mirror keeps its own tag wherever the night's dreams show, in
place of its "Dream n" tag: a pill like the others, saying it was added later, never that it is lesser (C138-I item
2).

While the mirror is prepared, the shimmer shows with AI·7's label (§5).

**The mirror heard** (MP·18). The voice reads exactly the words on screen, level, adding no feeling and no emphasis
(C132-I item 3). If the words leave the phone for that voice, Technology says so and the disclosure says so.

**The opening.** The framing lines and the clarifying questions in the app's look. The dreamer's answers in the
dreamer's bubbles. Interpret Now (W2) is a control: it looks like a control, never like a line of the app's.

**The section labels.** The line before the readings (MP·20), then "The Readings" (MP·36) above the readings; then
20px; then the image line. AI·11 above the series question. "Dreams" (MP·38) above the dreams on a night's page. Each
is a green pill with white text (§2.6; product/design-system.md). None ranks what sits under it. The arc has no
section label: it sits inside "The Readings" as the last reading (C138-I item 3). MP·37 is retired.

**The image line** (W8, W9). Composed each night (C134-I item 1). The app's look.

**The readings.** The tradition's look, whole, one bubble at a time (§2). A tradition's decline (W14) is in its own
voice, so in its reading's look, with its label (C134-I item 2). It is composed each night, with no stand-in (Lens
Practice §15 item 11; C149-I Q13). When every chosen tradition declines, the night shows the declines themselves and
nothing else marks it (Lens Practice §14 item 19; founder-ruled 2026-11-07). When readings fail and none passes, no
decline shows: the failure sentence (C4-P·6) does, in the app's look.

**The cap note** (W7), where a night has one. The app's voice, outside any reading's bubble: it speaks about the
readings, never as one (C135-I D6). The same in a thin night's look back (C11).

**The arc.** The last reading inside "The Readings", in one tradition's voice, so in the readings' look, the same white
bubble and size as every reading (L16; C138-I item 3). Its terracotta label names its kind beside its tradition, "The
Arc · {tradition}" (MP·52). Nothing marks it as a summary, a conclusion, or weightier than the readings above it.

**The press** (W16). Composed, with no fixed line: if it fails twice, it does not show that night, and the failure is
recorded (C140-I item 5). On a reading that is too comfortable, never on the dreamer's own words (L17). Its look is B1
(C135-I B): the app's own, plain and centred, at its place after the arc. Never set beneath a reading, never in a
tradition's look, never labelled as a press, never a visible step. It may speak of what a reading said; it never says
which tradition was right and never ranks one reading above another. No part of the app ever says a reading is true,
right or the one (C136-I), and the press is no exception. It cannot be marked (§2.5). It arrives as the app's words
do (§4).

**The series question** (W18). Composed, carrying the dreamer's word, spelled as they spelled it, and its tradition
(C134-I item 1). It has no stand-in: if composing fails, the question is silent that night, and nothing shows it
(founder-ruled 2026-11-03; notice to Interpretation). Asked through a named tradition (S2), so in the tradition's look, with its tradition's label. Above
it, its section label says the tradition asks (AI·11; C135-I D5), so it never reads as one of the night's readings,
which never ask (C134-I item 2). It cannot be marked (§2.5). Next passes it like any invitation (C131-I item 4). On a
night without it, nothing shows its absence (B3; P2).

**The close's invitations.** The app's look. The dreamer's response in the dreamer's bubbles. Next and Add to Journal
(W24) are controls, never lines.

**The reflection.** One paragraph in the app's voice, composed each night (C137-I item 1). It quotes lines word for
word, in quotation marks, each with where it comes from named: that is not a blend (C1). The app's own words in it say
only where and when, never a word that names a relation between the quoted lines. It begins from what the dreamer
wrote, never from the night. The dreamer's quoted words are in italics; a source's or a reading's are upright; each
quoted line of the dreamer's says whether it is from the dream or from what they wrote after the readings (C137-I item
2). Nothing more to draw. It is composed freely (C140-I item 3), built to C137-I item 1's rule, so a reflection that
names a relation is a defect, never the design. When it fails its check, the closed-set form stands in: the founder's
where-and-when phrases (AI·12 to AI·16; AI·17 is retired, since the reflection never says a line was marked, The
Close Draft 5, H8) and everything else quotation, placed as C129-I item 4 places them. The words
the check refuses are TECH·4 in product/founder-lists-for-technology.md. The same paragraph answers a write in an
unread night's space (product/main-path.md, The Journal; C138-I item 4).

**The respond state and the empty case** (W21, W22). Sentences (C137-I item 3; §6).

**The look back.** The whole night, every voice in its own look as it was shown, except the dream itself, which
shows in the dreamer's voice once confirmed (§2.7; C1; C8). A read night opens at the bottom, at the space, with the
night above, at rest: none of the reading's controls, no motion (product/main-path.md, The Journal).

## 4. How the words arrive

**The app's own words: word by word, the founder's trial for Cohort 0.** The app's lines appear one word at a time,
at one steady pace, the same every time, never faster or slower for effect. Interpretation's note sits beside this
(C132-I item 8): word by word makes the app the most alive voice on the page, where the Shared Core makes it the one
holding the traditions up.

**What would end it.** No preset signs. Word by word stays until the founder, using the app in Cohort 0, judges that it
is not right; his judgement is the test [RELAYED, Head of Product & Design, its message 5, G2]. If it ends, the app's
words arrive whole, as everything else does, and a later Product entry records it.

**The readings, the arc and the series question arrive whole,** one bubble at a time. Nothing performs them: no reveal,
no fade-up flourish, no sound, no haptic, no line saying they are ready (the Principles §4b).

**The dreamer's words** appear as they were given. Nothing animates them.

**The Word Animation setting** (MP·26) stops all of the app's own motion: word by word and the shimmer (§5). The
Principles' dated blocks allow this: a setting may change how the app's own words appear, and the shimmer is that
[RELAYED, Head of Product & Design, its message 3, item 11]. The phone's "Reduce motion" stops both as well. A screen
reader always gets each line, and each label, whole.

**The theme's change.** When Night/Day mode changes the theme while the app is open, the colours fade over five
seconds. With Word Animation off or "Reduce motion" on, they change at once (product/main-path.md, Settings).

## 5. Waiting: the shimmer

**What shows.** While the app works, a short label shows in the app's look, with a highlight sweeping across its text
for five seconds at most, after which the label stands still (below).
It shows work, never progress toward a reward. No bar, percentage, count, steps or checkmark. It never ends in a
flourish. When what the app made arrives, the label is gone and the content stands in its place (the Principles §4b).

**Where.** Where the next thing will appear, in the page's flow. One label at a time. Never on a journal row: the
journal shows no status on any row (S5; C131-I).

**The labels.** Each is a place in the register, founder copy, written at the copy pass (C11-P). The founder's drafts are kept
word for word and marked [founder's draft copy]; the register holds what each must do, its status and its flags.

| Place | When | The founder's draft |
|---|---|---|
| AI·1 | After "Looks right", before the first question | "Composing first question..." [founder's draft copy] |
| AI·2 | Between answers in the opening | "Preparing next question..." [founder's draft copy] |
| AI·3 | Before the readings | "Going through your answers and preparing the readings..." [founder's draft copy] |
| AI·4 | Before the close's first invitation | "Gathering the session..." [founder's draft copy] |
| AI·5 | After the dreamer hands over, while the reflections are made | "Composing reflection..." [founder's draft copy] |
| AI·6 | When an offline recording is transcribed, inside the night only | "Transcribing..." [founder's draft copy] |
| AI·7 | While the mirror is prepared | None: written at the copy pass |
| AI·8 | While a reflection is made in an unread night's space | None: written at the copy pass |

The gap between readings needs nothing.

**What a label names.** The app's work, never a mental act such as thinking or considering, which would imply a mind,
and never the dreamer's words (C135-I D3). Every label, now or later, is held to this: the register flags it on each
label's row, and its check catches the plainest words for a mental act in either language.

**A screen reader** hears the label once, whole, as a status message (WCAG 2.2, SC 4.1.3; the Principles §3). It is
not repeated while the wait lasts. When the content arrives, its announcement names which voice is speaking (§8).

**Motion, and SC 2.2.2.** The sweep starts by itself. A wait can last more than five seconds while the readings are
made, and the label sits beside the rest of the night, which can still be scrolled. W3C's "loading" exception covers
animation that is not shown alongside other content [READ at C9-P, W3C, Understanding SC 2.2.2], so it does not cover
this. A way to stop it is needed. The Word Animation setting and "Reduce motion" are such ways, and W3C counts one
control for all moving content as meeting the criterion [READ at C9-P, the same page]. But the setting sits on the
Settings page, so stopping the sweep mid-wait would mean leaving the night. **So the sweep runs for five seconds at
most, then the label stands still until the wait ends** (founder-agreed [RELAYED, Head of Product & Design, its message
5, G1]). A still label still says the app is working, and the sweep never lasts long enough to need a control
[RELAYED, WCAG 2.2 SC 2.2.2, its five-second condition, not read this session in the criterion's own text].

## 6. The silences, and the states

**Three silences told apart** (C4): the app is composing; nothing connected; nothing saved. "Nothing connected" means
W22, the empty case: nothing in the night could be set beside what the dreamer wrote. It never means no network.

**Composing.** The shimmer with AI·5's label, or AI·8's in an unread night's space. C4 says only "nothing connected"
is a sentence, and that the app never acknowledges separately from the reflection. A few words naming the app's work
are neither (C135-I D3).

**Nothing connected** (W22). A sentence on screen (C137-I item 3; C4), in the app's voice, standing where a reflection
would. It never reads as a judgement on what the dreamer wrote (C137-I item 3). This replaces the state shown by
design that C134-I item 3 allowed and the founder had agreed (G4).

**Nothing saved.** A failure, not a wait. Shown on screen by design, without a sentence, as C4 asks. It shows what the
app did, never anything about the dreamer (C4), and it is never quiet: the dreamer must see that their words were not
kept. A screen reader is told in EM·10's words.

**The respond state** (W21): the app will reflect; its part is done; this night is entered. A sentence on screen
(C137-I item 3; C12), a line above the input field in the button style, in the theme's main text colour. None of the
three reads as withdrawal, as something used up, or as a reward exhausted (C6).

**The search's empty result** (W25). A design state may replace the written line (C134-I item 6). If it does, it
tells "no night holds this" apart from searching and from failing, never implies the dreamer searched wrongly, and
never suggests another word. Its line stands until the founder chooses, in the copy pass [RELAYED, Head of Product &
Design, its message 5, G3].

**The two conditions on every state shown by design** (C134-I item 3), which now covers nothing saved and, if chosen,
W25:
1. No state looks like disapproval. Nothing dims, greys, strikes or marks the dreamer's words.
2. The click-through check C134-I item 3 asked for no longer applies: it was for W21 and W22 said by design alone,
   and they are sentences again (C139-I).

**The series question's silence** is never shown and never announced (B3; P2; C127-I I12).

## 7. Variance

Every night's readings differ (the Principles §4b). The look does not. Every night has the same containers, the same
weights, the same labels. Nothing suggests a verdict, a score, a rank or a favourite: no stars, no "strongest reading",
no reading set apart because the dreamer marked it. A marked sentence differs from an unmarked one only by the mark
(§2.5). Nothing in the look changes across nights with what the dreamer did (the Principles §4d).

## 8. Screen readers

- Every announcement about the night says which of the three voices is speaking, as the screen shows it (C127-I I12).
  The mirror is named as the app reading back (C135-I D4).
  Its words are places: C4-P·1 for the readings, EM·10 for the rest and for nothing saved, W21 and W22 as they stand
  on screen, and W25.
- **EM·10 is paused for Cohorts 0 and 1** (founder-ruled, C11-P). Until it is written, a screen reader hears what is
  visible: a tradition's label before its reading, the section labels, the mirror's tags and lines. The dreamer's
  bubbles and the app's own lines carry no visible label, so nothing tells them apart by voice. A dreamer-or-app label
  before each bubble is written before the app opens wider. C127-I I12 is met only in part, and Interpretation is told.
- Each line arrives whole, whatever Word Animation is set to. Each waiting label is heard once, whole.
- The dreamer's words and a source's quotation inside a reading can each be told from the tradition's prose, and the
  source is heard named (§2.3).
- Section labels are heard as headings; a tradition's label, as its name.
- The series question's silence is never announced.
- Announcements report what happened and nothing more. They never celebrate (the Principles §3, §4b).

## 9. What this leaves to Framer

The founder sets the values in Framer, and product/design-system.md records them, named by role: type, colour in both
themes, spacing, radius, bubble shapes, the shimmer's speed and direction, and every motion curve. They obey §2 to §8:
equal weight across traditions; the app's look never a reading's; italics only for the dreamer's quoted words; colour
never alone; the AA floor; Word Animation and "Reduce motion" honoured; nothing that performs or rewards. Framer's
variables, named by role, become Technology's design tokens.

## 11. For Technology

- AI·2's label is shown before the app knows whether the questions end (O9). Choosing it then is a cost, to be brought
  back.
- The mirror heard: whether its voice is made on the phone or off it (C132-I item 3).
- AI·6 shows inside the night only, never on a journal row.
- The sweep stops after five seconds at most, and the label stands still.
- Word Animation and the phone's "Reduce motion" both stop word by word and the sweep, and make the theme change at
  once.
- Status messages for every label and every arrival (SC 4.1.3), each naming its voice.
- The press never produces a sentence that declares a reading true, right or the one (C136-I).
