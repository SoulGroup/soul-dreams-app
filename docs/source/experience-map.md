# The Experience Map

*Every requirement, at the moment a dreamer meets it.*

**Product & Design**

*No matter where you are on your journey, the steps you take are equally important.*

**What kind of document this is.** A Playbook: current method, meant to change. It is replaced
when the founder's designs exist, and the old text is archived verbatim. Whether it is filed is
stated in product/product-change-record.md, never here.

**What it is for.** The design work happens in the founder's hands: on the wall, on paper, in
Figma. This map gives him what each moment must hold, what is refused there, and what is still
open there. It is organised by moment, not by screen. It does not say what the screens are, how
many there are, or where one ends.

**Working names are labels, not screens.** Inside some moments, requirements that cluster are
given a plain working name, such as "the mirror" or "the space", so the moment is easier to read.
A working name decides nothing. The founder replaces them.

**What governs.** The Requirements List (interpretation/requirements-list.md) governs every
requirement here. Where the list and its source differ, the source governs, or a later entry in
interpretation/change-record.md (the list's §0). This map points and restates nothing. If a row
here reads wrong against the list, the row is the defect. Everything beyond the reading is
governed by The Product Principles (product/product-and-design-principles.md), called "the
Principles" below.

**Scope.** Cohort 1 (C2-P item 5). An ID that no dreamer in Cohort 1 meets is placed as "Not met
in Cohort 1", with the ruling that says so. That counts as placed.

**The card sheet.** product/experience-map-cards.csv, filed beside this map. One card per row of
§3's table: the ID, its title, and the moment. The cards run by moment, M0 to M15 and then "Not met
in Cohort 1", and within a moment in the list's order, so each moment prints as one column of the
wall. Titles of the list's IDs are copied exactly from
the list's bold headings and place names, with markdown emphasis marks removed for print. Titles
of the nine handles are copied from their sources. Titles of the places this map finds are this
map's own (§4). The card sheet is for printing as post-its, or importing into FigJam.

## 1. The order, as ruled

**The night.** The list's §0, "The night, in order", as C124-I corrects it:

- Recording comes alone. It asks nothing and adds nothing. Recording and closing the app is a
  whole use (O1, O2).
- Being read begins only when the dreamer chooses, now or later. Its first step is the mirror
  (C124-I). The opening then runs in its one order (O12).
- The readings follow, then the band (B2), then the close, then the night in the journal.

**The moments.** A dreamer meets these. The codes are this map's own and decide nothing about
screens.

- **M0 Any moment.** What holds everywhere, and what the dreamer can reach from anywhere.
- **M1 First use.** From opening the app the first time to the first recording being possible.
- **M2 Recording.** The dreamer tells the night, by voice or by text, and may stop there.
- **M3 A capture-only night.** A night recorded and not read: as it closes, and as it is found later.
- **M4 Being read: the opening.** From the mirror to the bridge. It begins only when the dreamer chooses to be read.
- **M5 While the readings are made.** Between the bridge and the first reading.
- **M6 The readings.** The lenses, and the night's arc where there is one.
- **M7 The band.** Below the readings, before the dreamer's own turn.
- **M8 The close.** The look back, the space, the reflections, and the ending.
- **M9 The night entered.** Kept as it was shown. Only the space stays open.
- **M10 Coming back to the journal.** The nights, a night opened again, and search.
- **M11 A night read later.** A captured night, chosen for reading another time.
- **M12 A late telling.** Another dream told after the questions have begun.
- **M13 A failed reading.** A reading that does not complete: the service errors or times out, the connection drops partway, or the app stops. This is Product's definition, sent to Interpretation with I4.
- **M14 Offline recording.** Recording with no connection.
- **M15 Leaving.** Deleting a night, deleting the journal, removing the app.

M0 is not a time. It is what holds at every moment, and what is within reach from anywhere.

## 7. The check

From the repository root, `bash product/check-experience-map.sh` prints nothing when the map
and the card sheet are sound. It does the following:

- builds the required set from the list's own definitions, the nine handles, and the places
  this map finds in §4;
- reads only the ID column of §3's table;
- reports any ID missing from the map, and any ID in the map or the card sheet that is not
  defined;
- reports any row of §3 without exactly one card of the same ID and moment, and any card
  without a row;
- reports any card title that differs from its source, and a card sheet out of order;
- reports any "Not met in Cohort 1" row with no ruling in its note;
- reports any count at the top of this map that differs from what the map holds.

With `--count`, it prints the counts given at the top of this map.
