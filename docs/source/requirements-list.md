**How each requirement is laid out.**

- **Experience** — what the dreamer meets.
- **Source** — the document and heading, then any governing entry.
- **Recorded** — what is kept with the night, and what is counted only across all dreamers.
- **Never** — what must not happen. This is a pointer, not the whole list: each source's own
  "never" list is complete and is not copied here.
- **Words** — the places in §4 where founder-written words go.

A field that does not apply is left out.

**Numbering.**
- O — the opening.
- L — the readings.
- B — the band below the readings.
- C — the close.
- S — the series and the journal.
- X — across the whole app.
- W — places that need words.
- Q — open slots.

Numbers are this list's own, are permanent, and never move. A retired requirement keeps its
number.

**The night, in order** (Interpretation Framework, "The order a dreamer meets it"):

1. Capture.
2. The mirror.
3. What stood out.
4. The questions.
5. The stance.
6. The asking.
7. The bridge.
8. The readings, with the night's arc last where there is one.
9. The discernment pass. It is not a visible step.
10. The band:
    - the waking-life invitation, which is the grounding check;
    - the series question, on the few nights it runs.
11. The look back.
12. The space and the reflection.
13. The night in the journal.

## 1. The opening — before any tradition speaks

Owned by The Opening. Its "Behaviour to report to Product & Design" (twenty items) is the
primary source for this section. Its "What the opening never does" governs everything below
and is not copied here.

**O1 · Capture is the dreamer's, and it is silent.**
- **Experience:** The dreamer tells the dream, by voice or by text, and the app asks nothing
  and adds nothing. The capture-review screen keeps exactly what it has and gains nothing. A
  split the app detected is kept whether or not the dreamer confirmed it half-asleep. Tapping
  *keep it whole* never stores a night as one dream for good.
- **Source:** The Opening, "Phase one: the telling"; Behaviour item 4. Law document, §1,
  "Where it runs", *At capture*.
- **Never:** solicits more of the dream; asks a question at capture.

**O2 · Recording is a whole use; going on to be read is the dreamer's choice.**
- **Experience:** A dreamer may record where they are and close the app. Nothing treats that
  night as incomplete. Whether to go on from capture to be read is theirs to choose.
- **Source:** Shared Core; The Foundations §4.3, "A session is not a therapy session";
  Interpretation Framework, "The order a dreamer meets it" (C70-I item 5). How the choice is
  offered is The Opening's `[RELAYED, Interpretation Framework]`.
- **Never:** nudges a recording toward a session.

**O3 · The mirror reads the dream back in the dreamer's own words.**
- **Experience:** The read-back can be heard as well as read. It ends with at most one seam
  to respond to, and never with a statement of how the dream felt.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour items 1 and 6.
- **Never:** shows anything it concluded rather than transcribed; names the tone; supplies a
  word; smooths a contradiction or fills a gap.

**O4 · A night of several dreams is shown numbered and in order, and settled there.**
- **Experience:** The dreams appear numbered, in order. Nothing is said about how they
  relate.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour item 2; "Multi-dream nights".
- **Never:** characterises the sequence.
- **Words:** W6.

**O5 · The dreamer settles the record at the mirror, and the change holds.**
- **Experience:** There are three ways to correct the record. All are available and none is
  required:
  - edit the text directly;
  - tell the app what is structurally wrong;
  - give the words to use, which are applied literally.

  A merge or a split changes the night's dream count, and that is correct.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour item 3; "Multi-dream nights".
- **Recorded:** Every correction is recorded as a correction, never as an answer. It is not
  shown to the dreamer (Behaviour item 5). Corrections are capture layer (RQ1).
- **Never:** writes a sentence of its own from an instruction; requires a correction; invites
  more dream at the mirror.

**O6 · Right after the mirror, the dreamer is asked whether anything stood out.**
- **Experience:** The dreamer may name any number of images or moments, or none. On a night
  holding a single image the question is not asked. If the questions then bring more images
  in, it is asked once when they end.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour item 7.
- **Recorded:** The answer, verbatim, as an unordered set, with its position — after the
  mirror, or after the questions (The Opening, "What is handed on", item 3).
- **Never:** names an image; says which it would have chosen; ranks the answer.
- **Words:** W1.

**O7 · Questions come one per turn, and *I don't know* is a whole answer.**
- **Experience:** Questions can be answered by voice or by text. *I don't know* is never
  followed by the same question reworded. A question may be about an image the dreamer said
  stood out. Nothing asks the dreamer to explain why it did.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour item 8; "What the opening never does".
- **Never:** asks two things in a turn; asks *why*; asks about the dreamer's present state
  or body; anchors a question on anything but dream material; offers a meaning or a
  tradition's word.

**O8 · *Interpret now* always works.**
- **Experience:** On every question turn the dreamer can say or press *interpret now*. The
  questions end without judgement. Nothing about the control suggests the app is the
  interpreter.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour item 9.
- **Never:** asks a question after *interpret now*.
- **Words:** W2.

**O9 · The questions also end by themselves.**
- **Experience:** The questions end when two turns in a row bring nothing new. There is no
  fixed number. The questions have no cap in Cohorts 0 and 1.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour item 10; Interpretation Framework, "Who owns what"
  (C60-I (a); C70-I item 3).
- **Recorded:** Why the questions stopped, and the four counts (The Opening, "What is handed
  on", item 7). These are counted only across all dreamers (The Opening, "On watch", item 6).

**O10 · Once, at the close of the questions, the dreamer is invited to say what they made of
the dream when they woke.**
- **Experience:** The invitation is about what they made of it on waking, not how they feel
  now. *Nothing* is a complete answer.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour item 11.
- **Recorded:** The stance answer, verbatim, marked as waking material. It is outside the
  account and outside dream order (The Opening, "What is handed on", item 11).
- **Never:** follows it up.
- **Words:** W3.

**O11 · Then, once, the dreamer is invited to say what they want from this session.**
- **Experience:** *Nothing* is accepted without comment. If the dreamer chose *interpret
  now*, this invitation and O10's still appear, together, once. Doing nothing proceeds, and
  the dreamer is never held.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour items 12 and 13. What the asking may supply: L12.
- **Recorded:** The asking, verbatim, or *none* (The Opening, "What is handed on", item 5).
- **Words:** W4.

**O12 · The opening runs in one order.**
- **Experience:** The order is:
  1. telling;
  2. mirror;
  3. corrections;
  4. what stood out;
  5. questions;
  6. stance;
  7. asking;
  8. depth judgement;
  9. bridge;
  10. readings.

  The depth judgement is made only after the dreamer has finished correcting, and after all
  answers.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour item 14.
- **Recorded:** The depth score, with the two numbers it was computed from (The Opening,
  "What is handed on", item 6). It is never shown.
- **Never:** scores depth by anything other than its two inputs; scores before correcting is
  finished.

**O13 · A fragment is a whole session.**
- **Experience:** A one-image fragment is mirrored, however little there is. It gets:
  - no stood-out invitation;
  - one frame turn, and one seam turn if that yields;
  - the stance and the asking, offered once each;
  - the bridge, where it holds an image;
  - a reading sized to the material;
  - a note that says so plainly.
- **Source:** The Opening, "Thin dreams"; The Foundations §4.3.
- **Never:** suggests what might have been there; asks for associations on a fragment.
- **Words:** W7.

**O14 · One question about waking life, before the readings — the bridge.**
- **Experience:** The question is asked once, about the image the session is standing in
  front of. It asks what that image touches in the dreamer's waking life. It is asked on
  every night that holds an image, fragments included, and not on a night that holds none.
  Whatever the answer brings, the session stays with the dream.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour item 15; C80-I item 1 (founder-ruled 2026-09-30). The
  Foundations §4.5, dated block Item 5: no question names a period of the dreamer's life.
- **Recorded:** The bridge answer, verbatim, marked as waking material (The Opening, "What is
  handed on", item 11).
- **Never:** asks what the image means; names a period of the dreamer's life; follows up;
  drafts, words or weighs an action in the dreamer's life.
- **Words:** W5.

**O15 · Waking-life words stay waking-life words.**
- **Experience:** Anything the dreamer says about their waking life is only ever shown back
  as something they said about their waking life. It is never shown as something dreamt.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour item 16. How readings treat it: L12.
- **Never:** a tradition quotes it as dream material.

**O16 · The opening knows nothing of earlier nights.**
- **Experience:** Nothing the dreamer sees or hears in the opening is informed by their past
  nights. Nothing said after the mirror is read by a later night.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour item 17; "Series-blind by construction" (C67-I item 1);
  Recurrence & the Series RQ1.
- **Never:** asks a question that tracks a motif across nights.

**O17 · A dream told late becomes part of tonight.** *Head of Interpretation's ruling, open
to the founder.*
- **Experience:** A dreamer who tells another whole dream, unprompted, after the questions
  have begun has told tonight more of the night. It enters the account in their words, with
  its provenance marked late. Readings may read it.
- **Source:** C90-I item 1. The Opening's list of record kinds gains it at its next
  revision; this list carries it meanwhile.
- **Recorded:** As its own kind — a late telling. It is neither an answer nor a mirror
  correction. It is not capture layer.
- **Never:** recorded as an answer or as a correction; read by any later night for what
  returns (RQ1; C67-I item 4).
- **Open:** whether it is mirrored and settled, and how it takes its place among the night's
  numbered dreams. See Q4.
- **Evidence it occurs:** On one dreamer's seventeen sessions, late tellings occurred on
  three nights. Two of them carried open material the first telling did not (counts page,
  §1 and §3) `[READ]`.

**O18 · The opening records what it needs to be measured.**
- **Experience:** None. This is a record only.
- **Source:** The Opening, Behaviour item 20; "What is handed on", items 1 to 11.
- **Recorded:** What "What is handed on" lists. Two items there are kept as measures of the
  app, never as input, and are counted only across all dreamers:
  - volume in answers per image, marked confounded (C85-I);
  - returns, as presence only (The Opening, "Returns, for every image").
- **Never:** shown to the dreamer: the edit record, the depth score, returns, volume, or the
  markers on unresolved images.

## 2. The readings

Owned by Lens Practice. Its §14 "Behaviour" is the primary source. Its heading says *for the
founder to carry to Head of Technology*; it comes to Product, which designs it, and the
heading is flagged for Lens Practice's next draft (§10, F1).

**L1 · The readings stand in front of what the dreamer pointed at.**
- **Experience:** All the lenses tonight stand in front of the same image where three
  traditions have something to say about it. That image is what the dreamer said stood out,
  or what they went back to unasked. The session names that image, and says it is there
  because that is where the dreamer's own attention went. Where several images qualify,
  which one is chosen depends on nothing the dreamer said.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 items 3 and 13; The Opening, Behaviour item 18.
- **Recorded:** The chosen image (Lens Practice §14 item 11). Counted only across all
  dreamers.
- **Never:** reads as the app having chosen; implies the other images were dismissed.
- **Words:** W8.

**L2 · When no tradition can read the image the dreamer pointed at, the session says so.**
- **Experience:** The session names the image the dreamer pointed at and says no tradition
  reads it tonight. It then names the image it is reading instead.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 item 14.
- **Never:** quietly reads a different image.
- **Words:** W9.

**L3 · At most three lenses, set by tonight's material alone.**
- **Experience:** A night has at most three lenses. A fuller night gets a longer reading in
  one place, not more lenses. The count depends only on what tonight's dream leaves
  unresolved and what the dreamer asked. A dreamer's tenth session is read exactly as their
  first.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 item 2; Adaptive Scaffolding, "Removed: the complexity dial";
  The Foundations §4.3 and §4.4.
- **Recorded:** Whether the budget spilled (Lens Practice §14 item 11).
- **Never:** more lenses with familiarity, or fewer for a new dreamer.

**L4 · A dream that left nothing open, or was not reached, is told so.**
- **Experience:** A dream that left nothing open receives no lens, and the dreamer is told
  so, once. A dream the session did not get to is told that instead, in different words.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 item 1.
- **Recorded:** The list of dreams closed or not reached (Lens Practice §1, "Handed on").
- **Words:** W10, W11.

**L5 · Inside a lens, one paragraph per dream it can ground.**
- **Experience:** Each lens has a paragraph for each dream it has something grounded to say
  about, and nothing for the others. Dreams inside a lens stay in the order the dreamer left
  them.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 items 4 and 6.

**L6 · Every reading shows where it came from.**
- **Experience:** Every lens carries a why-line quoting the dreamer's own words. Every
  paragraph quotes its own dream's words.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 item 5. The quotation stays separable from the app's prose,
  and readings can be addressed span by span (The Close §15 `[RELAYED, The Close, citing
  Lens Practice §4.2]`).
- **Words:** W12.

**L7 · The order of the readings says nothing, and nothing says what it means.**
- **Experience:** Where the session reaches past the dreamer's main image to fill its lenses,
  the readings built on that main image come first. Otherwise the order carries nothing.
  Nothing anywhere says what the order means: no labels, no numbering, no *start here*, no
  ordering language.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 item 6; The Foundations §3, dated block Item 3; The Close
  H15.
- **Recorded:** The rendered order, with the night (The Close §7.1 B2). It is never read
  across nights.
- **Never:** tells a dreamer what the order means, in either direction, on any night.
- **Words:** P1 — a prohibition, not a line.

**L8 · The order never changes on a return.**
- **Experience:** A dreamer who reopens a session finds the lenses in the order they were in
  the first time. The order is the same on a re-read, on another device, and in the other
  language.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 item 12, which the source says must be built as an
  instrument; The Close H5.
- **Never:** recomputes a past order.

**L9 · A lens carries one tradition's name, and a waking-life account is never a lens.**
- **Experience:** Each lens is one tradition, under its own label. A lens never carries two
  names.
- **Source:**
  - Lens Practice §14 item 7.
  - Which traditions are served is the lens register's, not this list's. The Waking-Material
    Pass counts fourteen served of seventeen files `[READ, its "What it means in
    practice"]`.
  - Tibetan dream yoga is served in the private cohorts (C118-I item 3, founder-ruled). Its
    teachers or institutions are asked before any public use of the tradition or its label.
  - The Daoism label is work after the handoff (C118-I item 6), so see Q6.
- **Never:** the Tibetan tradition or its label used in anything public before those
  permissions are given.
- **Words:** W13.

**L10 · Disagreement is heard in a tradition's own voice.**
- **Experience:** Where two lenses disagree about the same image, the second says so in its
  own voice, in one sentence.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 item 8.
- **Recorded:** Each divergence (Lens Practice §14 item 11). Counted only across all
  dreamers.

**L11 · When a tradition does not read, it is the tradition declining.**
- **Experience:** A tradition may decline what it does not read, in its own voice. It may
  also say what an image would signify for a position it has not been told. Either way it
  is the tradition with the requirement, never the app being unable.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §15 items 11 and 12. Neither is in §14. It is carried here
  because a dreamer meets it.
- **Never:** invites the dreamer to supply what is missing; implies their account was
  inadequate; implies that knowing them over time would have helped.
- **Words:** W14, W15.

**L12 · What the dreamer said about waking life never decides a reading, and is never
quoted.**
- **Experience:** The bridge answer, the stance and the asking are never quoted in a reading.
  They never decide which tradition reads. A tradition whose own condition asks for waking
  material may read because of them. One that reads only because of them stands after every
  tradition the dream alone lets read.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 item 17; §1 item 11. C79-I item 1: the asking counts, under
  the same limits (founder-ruled). C79-I item 2: an answer supplies only what its question
  was built to draw. C80-I item 1: the bridge supplies the waking thing and never its
  recency (founder-ruled). C84-I item 4. Which traditions it reaches is the Waking-Material
  Pass's table.
- **Never:** prefers one tradition over another because of a waking answer; uses anything an
  answer carries beyond what its question drew. What else an answer carries is recorded as a
  substitute only and supplies nothing.

**L13 · The bridge's reach is watched, and it is provisional.**
- **Experience:** None directly. If the watch fires, the bridge stops feeding readings, and
  dreamers on later nights meet readings that no longer use it.
- **Source:** C80-I item 1 (founder-ruled). Its watch is held at Lens Practice §13 item 19.
- **Recorded:** Each night records whether a tradition read because of the bridge. It is
  stored with the night, counted only across all dreamers, and never shown (The Close §7.1
  B13; law document §4, "The per-night bridge record"). The tester disclosure says so (W27).
- **The watch:** clouding would look like this: on nights where the bridge let a tradition
  in, that reading leans on the dreamer's waking life rather than the dream. It is looked
  for first on fragments.
- **Never:** counted per dreamer; shown.

**L14 · A tradition's term is used bare only if the dreamer used it tonight.**
- **Experience:** If the dreamer used a tradition's term unprompted in this session, the app
  uses it bare. Otherwise it gives plain language first, with the term alongside. A fluent
  dreamer may be introduced to a term again on a night they did not say it.
- **Source:** Adaptive Scaffolding, "Re-scoped: vocabulary — within a session only".
- **Never:** remembers which terms a dreamer knows.

**L15 · How things are said may adapt within a session; what is said never does.**
- **Experience:** Tone, framing, pace and how much the app explains itself may follow what
  the dreamer is doing in this session. The uncomfortable thing is named either way. Every
  session starts from the same place.
- **Source:** Adaptive Scaffolding, "Re-scoped: delivery — within a session only"; The
  Foundations §4.2.
- **Never:** carries any setting from one session to the next.

**L16 · The night's arc: at most one, last of the readings, in one named tradition's voice.**
- **Experience:** On a night of more than one dream, the last reading may be a reading of
  how the dreams sit together. It is spoken by one named tradition, under its label, never
  by the app. On a night with no arc, nothing marks its absence. On a single-dream night
  there is none.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 item 10; §9; Recurrence & the Series RQ9 and RQ10.
- **Never:** reads across nights; summarises the readings above it; says that there is no
  arc.

**L17 · The discernment pass: the app may press once on a comfortable reading, unseen.**
- **Experience:** Between the last reading and the waking-life invitation, the session may
  press once on a comfortable reading. The dreamer never sees a step.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 item 15; §9A. Never cuttable (C44-I; Core Commitments §6.2).
  Its limits are the law document's, §2 (X5).
- **Recorded:** Which press it was, with the session (Lens Practice §14 item 15; its rotation
  watch is §13 item 18).
- **Open:** three questions are held, each with its owner (Q1). The slot is designed now with
  its function unsettled, as C54-I did for the series slot ("the slot specified and
  inoperable").
- **Words:** W16.

**L18 · A dreamer may mark a sentence while reading it.**
- **Experience:** The dreamer may mark a sentence in a reading, including its why-line, its
  divergence sentence, and the night's arc. The waking-life invitation and the series
  question cannot be marked. Nothing about the readings changes because of a mark — tonight
  or ever.
- **Source:** Lens Practice §14 item 16; §9B; The Foundations §8.
- **Recorded:** Marks, as presence on named passages. They may be counted by lens, by
  position or by kind, across a cohort and never per dreamer.
- **Never:** any figure of the form *what share of our readings resonate*; a mark rewarded,
  or read by selection, phrasing or order.

## 3. The band below the readings, and the close

Owned by The Close. Its "Behaviour to report to Product & Design" (thirteen items) is the
primary source. Its "What the close never does" governs everything here and is not copied.

**B1 · After the readings, the dreamer is asked how much of the dream their waking life
accounts for. Their answer is the grounding check.**
- **Experience:** The invitation comes once, below the readings, under its own heading. It is
  visibly not a lens. It names no image and no period of the dreamer's life, and never
  refers to the bridge. Nothing of the app's stands beside it. The dreamer answers with
  waking things, with *none of it*, with *all of it*, or with nothing. Their answer is shown
  back as their own words.
- **Source:** The Foundations §4.6, dated block Items 4 and 9; C76-I item 1 (founder-ruled):
  the dreamer's answer is the check, and the app renders none. Lens Practice §8 and §14
  item 9. The Close §3.1 and Behaviour item 1.
- **Recorded:**
  - The answer, in the dreamer's words, marked as theirs and as words about waking life (The
    Close §7.1 B4). It is never read by a later night (§7.2).
  - Whether the dreamer answered at all, as presence only. This is counted only across all
    dreamers, and reported but never optimised (Lens Practice §8, "Its instrument"; C83-I
    item 4).
- **Never:** any figure computed from what the answer says (The Close H13); a follow-up; a
  second ask; an app-side check before or after it.
- **Words:** W17.

**B2 · The band's order, and the series question's place in it.**
- **Experience:** The band runs in this order:
  1. the night's arc, where there is one, as the last of the readings;
  2. the waking-life invitation;
  3. on the few nights it runs, one question about what has returned, asked through a named
     tradition.

  The dreamer's turn still comes last. The app's final word is never a question.
- **Source:** The Close §6, constraint 1; Behaviour item 1. What the question is: S2 and S3.
- **Never:** more than three app-authored things in a row before the dreamer's turn. That is
  The Close's watch item 7, and it is not a rule to design around.

**B3 · When no series question is asked, nothing says so.**
- **Experience:** On a night with no series question, nothing in the ending looks as if
  something is missing. There is one silence, whatever its cause:
  - a first night;
  - nothing recurred;
  - no lens tonight can ask.
- **Source:** The Close, Behaviour item 2; §6, constraint 6. Recurrence & the Series RQ11 and
  Behaviour item 1.
- **Recorded:** That there was no question, never its cause (The Close §7.1 B5).
- **Never:** a line, placeholder or marker for the silence; a difference between one night
  and the next that announces a change.
- **Words:** P2 — no line, by decision.

**C1 · The night is held up whole — the look back.**
- **Experience:** The night is held up whole, in order, in four unblended voices:
  - the dreamer's words;
  - the app's own words;
  - the app reading through a named tradition;
  - a quoted source, where a reading carries one.

  It is complete and never condensed. The dreamer's words appear verbatim or not at all. The
  dreamer arrives at their own turn with the night above them, not at a re-reading. The full
  night is what they find when they come back.
- **Source:** The Close, Behaviour items 3 and 5; H1 to H4; §3.2 `[RELAYED, The Close
  Behaviour item 3, for §3.2's design]`.
- **Recorded:** The look back as rendered (The Close §7.1 B6).
- **Never:** summarises; condenses or smooths the dreamer's words; writes in the dreamer's
  person; arranges by anything but chronology; shows corrections as a diff.

**C2 · The dreamer's own words from the opening are part of the night.**
- **Experience:** The following are shown with the night, each as the dreamer's words:
  - what stood out;
  - what they made of the dream on waking;
  - what they wanted from the session;
  - what they said about waking life.
- **Source:** The Close, Behaviour item 4.

**C3 · The space is the dreamer's.**
- **Experience:** The space has no question above it and can be answered by doing nothing.
  A line of permission sits inside it and vanishes at the first keystroke. A promise
  persists while they write: their words stay with this night, and nothing in them shapes a
  later one. The space must read as theirs, not as a form.
- **Source:** The Close, Behaviour item 6; §3.3; H12.
- **Recorded:** What the dreamer writes, in their words, marked as theirs. Each write is kept
  separately and in the order written (The Close §7.1 B7). A write is a dreamer turn and is
  classified like every turn (law document §4).
- **Never:** reads what is written as input to any later night; computes any figure from it,
  except the raw count of whether the space is used at all (The Close H13).
- **Words:** W19, W20.

**C4 · Nothing happens until the dreamer hands over.**
- **Experience:** A deliberate act by the dreamer hands their writing to the app. Nothing
  fires on a pause. Three silences must be distinguishable:
  - the app is composing;
  - nothing connected;
  - nothing was saved.

  Only "nothing connected" is a sentence (W22). The save state describes what the app did
  and nothing about the dreamer.
- **Source:** The Close, Behaviour item 7; §3.4.
- **Never:** acknowledges separately from the reflection; shows a visible count of
  reflections left.

**C5 · What comes back is the dreamer's own sentence beside a sentence from the night — or
nothing, said plainly.**
- **Experience:** The reflection is the dreamer's own sentence, quoted, beside a sentence from
  the night. A few words of the app's say only where and when. It ends in a full stop. If
  there is nothing to set beside what they wrote, the app says so plainly.
- **Source:** The Close, Behaviour item 8; H6 to H11 and H17.
- **Recorded:** Each reflection as rendered, marked as the app's, with the two spans it
  quoted and where each came from. Each occasion that returned nothing (The Close §7.1 B8
  and B9).
- **Never:** asks a question; interprets; brings in anything from a prior night; uses the
  stance, the bridge, the waking-life answer or the asking as the night-side sentence.
- **Words:** W22.

**C6 · Three reflections, and then the app's part is done.**
- **Experience:** The app reflects at most three times per session. Then its part is done,
  and the night stays open and quiet. A line at the space says, in the moment, whether the
  app will still respond. It has three states: the app will reflect; the app's part is done;
  this night is entered.
- **Source:** The Close, Behaviour item 9; §3.3; §3.6 `[RELAYED, The Close §3.7, for §3.6]`.
- **Never:** reads as withdrawal, as something used up, or as a reward exhausted.
- **Words:** W21, W23.

**C7 · The dreamer ends the session when they choose.**
- **Experience:** From the moment the readings end, a control lets the dreamer end the
  session and enter the night. If they never use it, the app's part ends by itself a day
  after their last action, and nothing is announced.
- **Source:** The Close, Behaviour item 10; §3.7.
- **Recorded:** Which of the three paths ended the session, and when (The Close §7.1 B10).
  Never read across nights.
- **Never:** closes the night on the app's initiative; sends anything when the day runs
  out.
- **Words:** W24.

**C8 · A night is kept exactly as it was shown.**
- **Experience:** A dreamer returning a week later finds the night exactly as it was. That
  includes whatever they wrote and whatever the app set beside it. The lock fixes the night.
  The space stays writable indefinitely and is the only thing that does.
- **Source:** The Close, Behaviour item 11; H5 and H14; §3.8.
- **Never:** recomputes, regenerates or reorders anything a dreamer saw.

**C9 · A night has no label, badge or state a dreamer can read.**
- **Source:** The Close, Behaviour item 12; H16.
- **Recorded:** The night's internal state (The Close §7.1 B11). It is never shown.

**C10 · A capture-only night is whole.**
- **Experience:** A night where the dreamer recorded a few lines and closed the app has no
  look back. Its space is there when they open the night, and it answers. The window for
  reflections opens on the first write.
- **Source:** The Close, Behaviour item 13; §5.
- **Words:** W22 — written knowing the empty case is the normal case here.

**C11 · A thin night's look back is short because the night was short.**
- **Experience:** The cap note sits inside the look back.
- **Source:** The Close §4.
- **Words:** W7.

**C12 · On an entered night, a write is kept and nothing engages with it.**
- **Experience:** The dreamer can always tell, before writing, whether the app will still
  respond. That is the respond-state line (W21). It prevents silence on an entered night
  from reading as a judgement.
- **Source:** The Close §3.9.

## 4. The series, and the journal across nights

Owned by Recurrence & the Series. Its "Behaviour to report to Product & Design" (eight items)
is the primary source. Its "What this never does" governs everything here and is not copied.

**S1 · What counts as returning.** *Two halves, two authors.*
- **Experience:** A word returns when the dreamer used it on an earlier night and it appears
  again in the dreamer's telling tonight. On the earlier night, the word must have been used
  either for an image that night left unresolved, or to state a feeling the dream-self had.
  Only the dreamer's first telling and their own corrections at the mirror count, on both
  nights. Nothing said in answers counts, nothing told late counts, and nothing the app wrote
  counts.
- **Source:** Recurrence & the Series §4.1 `[PROVISIONAL]`; RQ1; RQ8. C90-I item 2: whether
  an image is unresolved is judged on the capture layer only. C118-I item 1 governs the next
  two points, and is carried here until Recurrence & the Series' next revision:
  - **Founder-ruled:** the broad reading. Every noun and adjective in the sentence naming the
    image counts, not only its head word.
  - **Head of Interpretation's reading of the founder's choice:** only the earlier night must
    hold the image unresolved.
- **Cohort 0 watch, attached here:** whether ordinary words fire as returns. On one
  dreamer's material, this reading fired on 8 of 12 nights past the fifth (counts page §2,
  the BROAD row, telling alone) `[READ]`. What recurred there was mostly ordinary: common
  nouns, and general-purpose adjectives or adverbs (counts page §2, "What it shows", item 3)
  `[READ]`. One dreamer only, and consistent-with, never confirming. The founder has been
  told `[RELAYED: Head of Interpretation 2026-10-07]`. This is the case PR2 predicted, in the
  wording PR2 used: a recurrence that is *trivial*, *an ordinary noun the dreamer does not
  consider a motif* (Recurrence & the Series §14).
- **Recorded:** Nothing. The app holds no object about recurrence. It is computed once per
  night and leaves behind only the question it produced (RQ2).
- **Never:** synonym, paraphrase or theme; weighting by how much was said or how often
  (RQ4); a significance filter added to answer complaints of triviality (Recurrence & the
  Series §15).

**S2 · The question about what returned.**
- **Experience:** It is one question, about one word, through one named tradition. It
  contains the dreamer's own word, spelled as they spelled it, and no number of any kind.
  On their tenth session a dreamer meets a question of the same length as on their second.
- **Source:** Recurrence & the Series, Behaviour items 2 and 5; §7.2; RQ3, RQ6 and RQ7. The
  Close §6, constraints 2, 4 and 9. The word is chosen seeded and arbitrary from the night's
  own identifier (§5.1).
- **Recorded:** The question as rendered, with its tradition (The Close §7.1 B5).
- **Never:** a count, a date, a number of nights, a comparison between nights; any
  statement that something is changing; any suggestion that the return means anything (§7.3).
- **Words:** W18.

**S3 · Only a tradition that holds an account of recurrence may ask.**
- **Experience:** The question is asked only through one of tonight's lenses whose file
  declares an account of something recurring across separate nights. A dreamer is asked
  about a feeling they named only through a tradition whose account reaches a returning
  feeling. They are never asked to compare how they felt on two nights.
- **Source:** Recurrence & the Series, Behaviour item 8; §5.2. The Close §6, constraint 3.
  - Three files declare (recurring-image pass, "The count") `[READ]`.
  - None is established to reach a returning feeling (C83-I item 2), so the feeling half is
    silent on every night.
  - The question runs on few nights (C61-I).
- **Open:** the Freudian source work after the handoff could move one row (C84-I item 1;
  C118-I item 6). See Q5.

**S4 · Reopening a night shows exactly what was shown, the question included.**
- **Source:** Recurrence & the Series, Behaviour item 3; The Close §6, constraint 7.

**S5 · The journal list never grades, and never shows recurrence.**
- **Experience:** In the list of nights, a dreamer cannot tell which nights they wrote in,
  how long they stayed, or which nights are connected to any other. A capture-only night and
  a night with three reflections look the same. There is no view where a dreamer sees their
  series.
- **Source:** Recurrence & the Series, Behaviour item 4; §8.1; RQ12 and RQ13. The Close
  §3.8.
- **Never:** a badge, status, count, thread, link, grouping, highlight, tag, or filtered view
  offered by the app.

**S6 · The dreamer can search their own journal for their own word.**
- **Experience:** The dreamer types a word. The app shows the nights containing it, in date
  order, with no ranking. The search spans everything the dreamer wrote, the space included.
  When a word appears on one night only, the result says so without implying they searched
  wrongly.
- **Source:** Recurrence & the Series §8.2 (C56-I item 4, founder-ruled); RQ5.
- **Never:** suggests, completes or lists a word — a permanent refusal (RQ15); orders by
  relevance.
- **Words:** W25.

**S7 · The app never proposes a word, a connection or a pattern.**
- **Source:** Recurrence & the Series, Behaviour item 6; §12.

**S8 · Nothing a dreamer writes after the readings changes anything on a later night.**
- **Source:** Recurrence & the Series, Behaviour item 7; §3.6 and §3.7 `[RELAYED, its
  headings]`; The Close H12 and §7.2.

## 5. Across the whole app

**X1 · Crisis resources are reachable from the app at all times, and nothing offers them.**
- **Experience:** The dreamer can fetch the resources from anywhere in the app. The app never
  offers them, even on a turn where the classifier fires.
- **Source:** Law document, "Standing, from the first dreamer who is not the founder"; Core
  Commitments §6.3, never cuttable. The content is verified current before Cohort 1 (C63-I
  item 9).
- **Never:** called by a classifier or by any decision the app makes.
- **Words:** W26.

**X2 · Before anything is recorded, a tester reads one disclosure and gives one consent.**
- **Experience:** Before recording anything, the tester reads one document, and gives consent
  in it, the beta access consent included.
- **Source:** Law document, "Standing"; tester disclosure specification §2 to §4 (C64-I item
  4).
- **Never:** a separate consent; a disclosure that reassures, softens the absence, promises a
  future in which the app notices, or claims anonymity.
- **Words:** W27.

**X3 · Every dreamer turn is classified for distress; nothing the dreamer meets changes.**
- **Experience:** Nothing. Detection runs from Cohort 0 and is recorded. Consequence is
  deferred, and every session proceeds as if the classification were clear. No cohort is
  held back waiting for it.
- **Source:**
  - Law document §1 (C51-I item 1).
  - The Foundations §6, dated block Item 6.
  - The Opening, Behaviour item 19.
  - The Close §8.
  - Every capture, every turn, and every write in the space is classified.
- **Recorded:** Each firing is stored with the night it occurred in and counted only as a
  population. Nothing indexes firings by dreamer. The rate computation takes no dreamer
  identifier. In Cohort 1 the rate is one cohort total, never sliced by time or by tester
  (law document §4; C62-I; C63-I items 3 to 5).
- **Never:** shown; used as a score; read across sessions; claimed in any copy as guaranteed
  detection, or as absent.
- **The residual, stated:** the app cannot stop, because it does not know (C51-I item 2). It
  is answered by the disclosure (X2) and by measuring the rate.

**X4 · Distress firings are kept only until the review of the cohort they occurred in.**
- **Experience:** The tester is told how long firings are kept, and the text promises nothing
  beyond it.
- **Source:** C118-I item 2 (founder-ruled), on C84-I item 5. The tester disclosure
  specification §2 item 2 still says no ruling states this; it is carried here until it is
  updated (§10, F6).
- **Never:** kept past that review; promised beyond it.
- **Words:** W27.

**X5 · Friction never presses on the person.**
- **Experience:** The app may press on readings, closures and defences, and never on the
  person. Several limits hold wherever the app presses:
  - The app never presses toward worthlessness.
  - It never presses on an account of harm, or on fresh grief.
  - It never advises, endorses or weighs a real-world action, and never acts in the
    dreamer's waking life on their behalf, even when asked.
  - Figures in a dream are spoken of as figures, never as claims about real people.
  - "No" is final.
  - Warmth is never withheld as technique.
  - Discomfort never earns praise and is never gamified.
- **Source:** Law document §2, "The line friction cannot cross". Every question and
  invitation is a press, before the readings and after, fragments included (C83-I item 7).
  The Foundations §4.1 and §4.2.
- **Never:** an unwelcome reading shaped like a judgement of the dreamer's worth; a
  question about what to do in life.

**X6 · What the app is not for is said plainly.**
- **Experience:** A tester is told, at the point they learn what the app will not do, that
  this is not therapy and that there are things it is not meant to hold. It is not a warning,
  and it does not limit what may be written.
- **Source:**
  - Tester disclosure specification §2 item 6.
  - Law document §2, "'Not therapy' as hard boundary, not disclaimer".
  - Public language is governed by law document §3. Its rule binds every public surface
    this list touches.
- **Never:** diagnoses; frames a session as treatment; claims an outcome.
- **Words:** W27; W28 for later cohorts.

**X7 · The app never learns the dreamer, and never asks them back.**
- **Experience:** A tenth session is read exactly as a first. There are no levels, no
  progress indicators, and no signal to the dreamer about where they are. There are none of
  the following:
  - streaks or badges;
  - achievements;
  - notifications asking anyone to come back;
  - win-back messages;
  - anything whose timing adapts to what a dreamer does.

  No measurement of any kind is shown to a dreamer. One allowance: a reminder the dreamer
  sets themselves, at a time they choose. It never changes on its own and never mentions
  absence.
- **Source:** The Foundations §4.4 and dated block Item 7; Adaptive Scaffolding, "Invisible
  by design"; Core Commitments §3, prohibition 3, and §4.1.
- **Never:** a cross-session record about the dreamer, even one nothing acts on.

**X8 · No verdict, anywhere.**
- **Experience:** Nothing in the app says what a dream means. There are no confidence
  scores and no ranking numbers. Fit is expressed in words and in order only, and the order
  is silent (L7).
- **Source:** The Foundations §3; Shared Core, "What we never do".

**X9 · Voice is transcribed, then the audio is gone.**
- **Experience:** The dreamer is told plainly what happens to their voice.
- **Source:** Law document §4, "Voice audio is transient". The sentence is owed before the
  screen it sits on ships.
- **Recorded:** The transcript. Audio retention is 0 after transcription by default.
- **Words:** W29.

**X10 · The dreamer is told, in one plain sentence, who sees their words and under what
retention.**
- **Source:** Law document §4, "No training on user data". User content is never used to
  train or fine-tune models.
- **Words:** W30.

**X11 · Deletion is real.**
- **Experience:** Deleting a night or the account removes it and everything derived from it.
  Recurrence leaves no orphan behind, because nothing about recurrence is stored.
- **Source:** Law document §4, "Deletion is real and cascades"; Recurrence & the Series §9
  `[RELAYED, its heading; §15 calls the claim one about a design not yet built]`.

**X12 · The app asks for nothing it does not use.**
- **Experience:** No contacts, no location, and no analytics that collect events wholesale.
  Nothing is collected without a use.
- **Source:** Law document §4, "Data minimization at capture".

**X13 · Every dreamer-facing line exists in English and in Swedish, each written
independently.**
- **Experience:** A dreamer in either language meets lines written by a person in that
  language. The order of the readings is the same in both (L8).
- **Source:** Shared Core; C118-I item 5; Lens Practice §14 item 12.
- **Never:** machine-translated; drafted by a model or a chat.

## 6. What a night keeps, and what may be counted — an index

This is an index into the requirements above, not a second statement of them. The complete
list of what a night records is The Close §7.1. What a later night may read is Recurrence &
the Series §3.

**Kept with the night, and shown as the dreamer saw it:**
- the account and its corrections (O5);
- what stood out, the stance, the asking and the bridge answer, as the dreamer's words (C2);
- the readings and their order (L7, L8);
- the waking-life answer (B1);
- the series question (S2);
- the look back (C1);
- what was written in the space (C3);
- each reflection (C5).

**Kept with the night and never shown:**
- the edit record, the depth score, why the questions stopped, returns, volume in answers,
  and the markers (O18);
- the chosen image, whether the budget spilled, and the divergences (L1, L3, L10);
- the discernment pass's press (L17);
- whether a tradition read because of the bridge (L13);
- how the session ended (C7);
- the night's internal state (C9);
- distress firings, kept only until their cohort's review (X3, X4).

**Counted only across all dreamers — never per dreamer, never as a trend about a person:**
- the four counts of why the questions stopped (O9);
- returns and volume (O18);
- marks, by lens, position or kind (L18);
- whether a tradition read because of the bridge (L13);
- whether the waking-life invitation was answered (B1);
- divergences, the chosen image and spill (L1, L3, L10);
- the distress firing rate (X3);
- the fluency signals.

The fluency signals are read only from the dreamer's turns before any tradition speaks. They
are measurement, never input (Adaptive Scaffolding, "The fluency signals"; law document §4).

**Never computed, by anyone, for anything:**
- any figure of the form *what share of our readings resonate* (The Foundations §8);
- any figure from what the waking-life answer says, or from the space beyond whether it is
  used at all (The Close H13);
- any figure about a dreamer from recurrence (Recurrence & the Series §12);
- any per-person trend across sessions (Adaptive Scaffolding, "RETIRED").

**Which numbers may never be a target** is Core Commitments §3 and §3.1, pointed at here.
Where Core Commitments and The Foundations §8 differ, §8 governs. A number may be reported
as evidence without being optimised as a goal (Shared Core). No measurement is ever shown to
a dreamer (X7).

Each open slot is something a screen must hold whose content or function is not yet settled.

Product's. The precedent for designing a slot whose function is unsettled is C54-I ("the
slot specified and inoperable").
