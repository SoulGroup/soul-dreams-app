# Founder Lists for Technology

*The words the app's checks refuse or trim, in English and in Swedish, and which lines each list checks.*

**Product & Design**

*No matter where you are on your journey, the steps you take are equally important.*

**What kind of document this is.** A Playbook: current method, meant to change. When a list changes, this file is replaced and the old text archived verbatim. Whether it is filed is stated in product/product-change-record.md, never here. It is part of the handover to Technology (kit item 9).

**Whose it is.** TECH·1 and TECH·2 are the founder's, approved by him on 2026-11-03 [RELAYED, Head of Product & Design]. TECH·3, TECH·4 and TECH·5 are approved by Head of Interpretation, now and as they grow, by the founder's delegation: no dreamer sees them, so they are rules for the app's checks, not dreamer-facing words (C142-I item 0). No dreamer ever sees them. They are not places and hold no lines: product/places-register.md holds the lines.

**How every list is matched.** Each entry is matched as a whole word or a whole phrase, ignoring case. Each language's list applies only to lines in that language. Quotations are never checked: neither the dreamer's quoted words nor a source's. A Swedish entry matches only the form written here; another form of the same word is a separate entry, added to the list when it is needed.

**How a list grows.** In Cohort 0, a relation the reflection's check missed puts its words on TECH·4, in every language. Only the words are filed, never the reflection (C140-I item 3). Any other change goes through Product & Design and is filed; changes to TECH·3 to TECH·5 are approved by Head of Interpretation.

**Every list is a floor.** A word on a list is refused every time; what a list does not hold is caught only by a model check, which fails sometimes (C140-I item 3; C142-I).
